# Nen map completion boundary

This is the final dedicated expansion phase for the fullscreen Nen relationship map.

## Maintained coverage

- Six hexagonal Nen category nodes.
- Three secondary pure-type examples per category.
- Exact midpoint and leaning placements from the Togashi exhibition chart.
- Maintained ability, system, or aptitude records for every idle and secondary user.
- Activation, effect, cost or restriction, natural category, and conservative supporting-category metadata.
- Category focus that reveals placement users and a compact ability rail without changing the idle map.
- Pan, zoom, fit, reset, pointer, keyboard, inspector, and scaled-canvas QA contracts.

## Accuracy rules

- Natural category is not treated as proof of every category used by an ability.
- Midpoint or leaning placement is not treated as a second natural type.
- Supporting categories are included only when the approved references or strong manga-grounded analysis support them.
- Non-named phenomena such as Komugi's Gungi aptitude, Meruem's Aura Synthesis, and Alluka/Nanika's request system are explicitly maintained as aptitude or system records rather than falsely presented as canonical named abilities.
- Visible source-status badges, chapter-boundary UI, translation-comparison UI, and background redesign remain outside the agreed scope.

## Future maintenance

After this phase, Nen should receive only factual corrections, newly confirmed canon records, bug fixes, and small presentation refinements. Major product work moves to the Voyage Timeline and then the Succession Contest arc as a whole.
