## Method

Each chapter was assigned one of four review outcomes:

1. **Evidence:** one or more spatial, route, operational, temporal, scale, access, visual, or contradiction claims were captured.
2. **Context:** the chapter informs the voyage or occupancy but adds no dependable geometry.
3. **No relevant evidence:** the chapter was reviewed and contained no Black Whale reconstruction evidence.
4. **Unavailable:** the requested chapter has no released canon Hunterpedia record.

Claims are stored atomically. A single room description may therefore produce separate records for its doors, furniture, adjacency, access conditions, temporal state, contradictions, and modeling limitations.

The corpus stores metadata, URLs, filenames, and derived observations. It does **not** redistribute manga pages.
