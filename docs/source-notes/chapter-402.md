# Chapter 402 source note

## Source boundary

The modern Chapter 402 packet uses the current user-supplied Hunterpedia-style synopsis provided on 2026-08-10 as its sole substantive story source. No Chapter 402 title is supplied in the current synopsis, so no title or Japanese title is invented. No Chapter 403+ story claim is imported to fill a Chapter 402 gap.

Chapter 402 spans Voyage Day 10 and Voyage Day 11. Exact supplied anchors are **Day 10 at 1:30 p.m.**, **Day 11 at 6:00 a.m.**, **Day 11 at 8:00 a.m.**, and **Day 11 at 8:50 a.m.** The chapter’s presentation is intentionally non-linear: after the Day 11 8:00 a.m. Tserriednich scene, the synopsis cuts back to the earlier 6:00 a.m. Melody/Kacho-form scene. The maintained event order preserves presentation order while each event separately retains its actual day/time anchor. Intermediate times are not invented.

## Zhang Lei / Tubeppa / Woble endgame compact

Tenftory tells Zhang Lei that if Zhang Lei, Tubeppa, and Woble are the final three princes, Tubeppa and Woble are prepared to renounce in Zhang Lei’s favor and put that commitment in writing. In return, Woble asks for Sakata and Hashito to extend their support indefinitely.

The archive records the reported political commitment. It does **not** declare that succession-contest surrender or renunciation is legally valid. Zhang Lei himself questions whether surrender is possible under the rules, and the chapter does not resolve that point.

Zhang Lei gives Tenftory a second Guardian Coin, promises an additional reward if Tenftory reaches an unspecified number of coins, and tells him carrying the coins will bring fortune. Zhang Lei privately notes that his expected ten-day development did not occur and considers whether the coins may fail to show their real power while he personally retains them. These are Zhang Lei’s statements and hypotheses. Chapter 402 does not independently demonstrate a new fortune effect, holder threshold, or confirmed distribution trigger.

## Tubeppa / Rihan / Guardian Spirit Beast

Tubeppa welcomes the alliance report involving Kurapika, Woble, Oito, and Zhang Lei’s information-sharing representatives. She hopes public closeness with Zhang Lei will redirect Benjamin’s attention away from her and instructs Maor and Longhi to emphasize that closeness in front of Benjamin’s soldiers.

Rihan directly sees aura gather behind Tubeppa and sees her Guardian Spirit Beast appear, croak, and emit fumes. The appearance itself is confirmed. Rihan’s conclusion that the timing after the peace-treaty/alliance development may mean the beast uses a conditional-type ability is **Rihan’s deduction**, not a confirmed trigger or ability classification. The fumes are not assigned an invented effect.

## Balsamilco pathological-weapon boundary

Balsamilco tests a gas-emission mechanism built into his right shoe, opens a secured box containing three vials of a pathological weapon previously created by the Kakin military, loads one vial, and resolves to erase Halkenburg.

The supplied synopsis states that the agent’s proteins denature in air in less than thirty seconds; inhalation before that can initially resemble stomach flu and later cause fatal multiple-organ failure in roughly half a day; decomposition is accelerated and ordinary decay bacteria can eliminate the remaining trace during autopsy. These properties are recorded only as the supplied description of this specific weapon. No additional manufacturing, culturing, dosing, optimization, or deployment procedure is invented.

Most importantly, Chapter 402 shows **preparation and intent**, not confirmed exposure. Halkenburg is not marked infected, poisoned, incapacitated by this weapon, or killed at the Chapter 402 boundary. Melody later knows Halkenburg has fallen ill, but Chapter 402 does not establish that Balsamilco’s agent caused the illness.

The weapon’s Kakin-military development history also does not prove that the military institution as a whole ordered Balsamilco’s specific anti-Halkenburg operation.

## Tserriednich / Salkov Zetsu boundary

At 8:00 a.m. on Day 11, Salkov times Tserriednich entering Zetsu at **9.67 seconds**. Tserriednich can maintain the observed Zetsu state while running on a treadmill, playing a phone game, and talking with Vantine. Salkov regards the learning rate as extraordinary.

Salkov then tries to explain Theta’s earlier account. He considers several possibilities: a special ability activating during Zetsu; the jester-like Nen beast storing aura under normal conditions and activating in Zetsu; or Theta having been manipulated after an attack by Tserriednich’s Guardian Spirit Beast and therefore giving false testimony. Salkov still cannot see the jester-like beast and explicitly lacks enough information to decide.

The archive therefore does **not** choose among Salkov’s models, does not redefine Parallel Future from this scene, and does not establish that the jester-like beast stores aura or that Theta was manipulated.

## Melody detention / Vantine recruitment thread

Vantine reports that Justice continues to detain Melody over suspected involvement in the escape attempt and that Benjamin appears to share the suspicion. Tserriednich orders Vantine to continue meeting Melody, learn what she is thinking, and try to win her over before Benjamin does.

This establishes Tserriednich’s recruitment objective, not Melody’s willingness to join him.

## Fugetsu shoulder mark

At 6:00 a.m. on Day 11, Kacho-form shows Melody a photograph of a mark on Fugetsu’s right shoulder blade. Kacho-form says it was not present before the escape attempt. The physical mark and Kacho-form’s timing report are recorded.

Melody first considers an injection site, then develops a Nen-based explanation after Kacho-form notes that Fugetsu was immediately taken into Justice custody after returning. Melody proposes that some Nen abilities use multistep activation and that Fugetsu may have been marked by a trap.

That is **not** a confirmed mechanic. Chapter 402 does not identify the user, official ability name, Nen category, exact activation sequence, or purpose of the mark.

## Repeated-use / addiction / enemy-door theory

Kacho-form remembers Fugetsu saying she can use Magical Worm repeatedly. Melody suggests testing the ability and predicts that two doors may appear, with one being an enemy trap. Melody also describes Fugetsu’s recent behavior as resembling addiction and changes her earlier negotiation-hostage theory into a new model: an indiscriminate trap could cause victims to keep using an enemy ability and become progressively weaker.

These are investigative hypotheses. The archive does **not** establish that an enemy ability is actually creating a second door, that Fugetsu is literally drugged, that repeated legitimate Magical Worm use is causing her decline, or that an enemy ability is responsible for her belief that she can use Magical Worm repeatedly.

Melody’s warning that Fugetsu may not last until the following weekend is preserved as Melody’s assessment, not a narrator-certified prognosis.

## Luzurus suspect boundary

Kacho-form suspects Luzurus because of his drug dealings and Cha-R sponsorship and guesses that his Guardian Spirit Beast may be responsible for Fugetsu’s condition. Melody notes that even if Luzurus is involved he may not be consciously directing the attack.

No Chapter 402 observation identifies Luzurus or his Guardian Spirit Beast as the culprit. The later 8:50 a.m. planning scene explicitly acknowledges that **Luzurus may not be responsible** and that acting quickly would leave more time to find the real culprit if the theory is wrong.

The proposed poisoning, strangling, forced transport, and apparent-accident scenarios are therefore stored as an operation aimed at an **unconfirmed suspect**, not as punishment of a proven attacker. No attack or death of Luzurus occurs in Chapter 402.

## Magical Worm destination and route boundary

Kacho-form reports several Chapter 402 tests. A desired destination outside the Black Whale does not produce a door. Fugetsu can connect to the ship’s emergency lifeboat area and the inside of the first lifeboat.

Kacho-form also describes the observed door-closing sequence: the Outgoing Door closes when Fugetsu enters the tunnel, and the Door of Return closes when Kacho-form enters on the return trip. Kacho-form speculates that other people may be able to enter and leave if Fugetsu and Kacho-form are not already inside. Chapter 402 does **not** show that third-party access test being completed, so broad third-party access remains unresolved.

Melody identifies another practical constraint: to route to Luzurus’s master bedroom, Fugetsu must first physically visit that destination. Kacho-form agrees, and Kaiser turns the prince-letter operation into a way to obtain access to multiple possible destinations. The archive records prior visitation as the group’s operationally established destination prerequisite in Chapter 402 without inventing a complete universal topology rule beyond what the scene supplies.

## Kaiser / Melody trust boundary

Kaiser volunteers to carry an incapacitated Luzurus through the proposed Magical Worm route and helps design an apparent-accident cover story and Justice-alibi structure. When Melody asks whether he understands the personal risk, Kaiser says his motive is not merely romantic: he believes Fugetsu should become king, thinks Kacho-form can help shield her from Seiko’s interference, and says he wants to move the world toward a gentler order even if doing dirty work disqualifies him from that ideal world.

Melody continues listening to his heartbeat and detects no change. She nevertheless remains unable to identify his true motive and keeps open the possibility that Kaiser, or whoever may be manipulating him, is using the group. She continues the relationship because it currently helps protect Fugetsu.

The archive therefore records Kaiser’s ideological declaration as his stated motive, not independently proven sincerity. Kaiser is **not** classified as a Manipulator, self-manipulated target, or confirmed enemy agent in Chapter 402.

## Benjamin / Fugetsu spirit interaction

Fugetsu personally delivers Kacho’s letter to Benjamin under Justice escort. Benjamin can see the ghostly spirits around Fugetsu. As she approaches, Benjamin’s Guardian Spirit Beast screeches and the spirits visibly disperse. That disappearance is directly observed and is recorded as a real interaction.

Benjamin then concludes that they are low-level evil spirits that plague weakened people, rejects the idea that this is a scheme by Fugetsu’s Guardian Spirit Beast, connects Fugetsu’s weakness to despair after Kacho’s loss, and believes she will not last another ten days. Those diagnostic and causal claims are **Benjamin’s assessment**. Chapter 402 does not independently prove the spirits’ taxonomy, original source, or Fugetsu’s remaining lifespan.

The Guardian Spirit Beast’s observed screech/dispersal does not establish a universal exorcism ability, exact anti-spirit category, permanent cure, or effectiveness against stronger curses.

## Special Martial Law boundary

During the Benjamin meeting, Kaiser deliberately raises stronger search authority and allows Benjamin to complete the thought. Benjamin says the court case concerning royal privilege versus military authority is still ongoing and explicitly states that the **current situation does not meet the national-crisis conditions required for martial law**.

Kaiser privately believes his leading question caused Benjamin to reveal that he is waiting for the right opportunity to implement martial law. That intention-reading is Kaiser’s inference. The threshold statement is direct; the hidden plan is not.

Special Martial Law is **not declared or active** in Chapter 402.

## Kacho final-letter operation

Kacho-form and Kaiser use letters presented as Kacho’s handwritten final messages to justify Fugetsu personally visiting prince quarters. Kaiser explains that Justice has separately gathered sensitive insider information about the princes and proposes using that information in the letters to shake the recipients while simultaneously gaining access and observing reactions.

The operation reaches Benjamin, Tubeppa, Tyson, and Luzurus in the supplied Chapter 402 synopsis. Luzurus unexpectedly gives his letter to Kaiser, hears Kaiser characterize the material as rumors, and infers that different princes probably received different “social bomb” messages. Luzurus’s reconstruction is his own deduction.

The synopsis explicitly supplies an anti-Tserriednich line in Luzurus’s letter: that Tserriednich is the prince Kacho would not want on the throne. It also supplies Melody’s later description of the Halkenburg letter as containing information about Benjamin’s secrets, Tserriednich’s depravity, the truth of Halkenburg’s birth, and information capable of shaking the contest’s foundations.

The archive records those contents as material intentionally placed in the operation. It does **not** automatically certify every allegation in the letters as independently proven fact merely because a letter contains it.

## Basho haiku charm boundary

After the Luzurus visit, the spirits around Fugetsu become active and then subside. Basho approaches, identifies himself as Melody’s colleague, and gives Fugetsu a good-luck charm containing a haiku. Basho internally expects that low-level evil spirits will not approach her for a while while she has it.

The charm handoff, the observed spirit reduction, and Basho’s expectation are recorded. The current synopsis does **not** supply an official ability name, Nen classification, precise duration, maximum range, universal spirit category, activation poem requirements, or full mechanics for this charm. None are invented.

## Halkenburg rumble / illness boundary

At 8:50 a.m., Melody brings up a rumbling she heard or felt directly above the Justice Bureau and guesses it belonged to Halkenburg or his Guardian Spirit Beast because he was the only other prince on Tier 2. She also knows Halkenburg has fallen ill and asks Kaiser to determine whether the earlier event was planned.

The rumble’s source and the illness’s cause remain unresolved. Chapter 402 does not connect the illness to Balsamilco’s prepared pathological weapon merely because both threads coexist in the chapter.

## Halkenburg and Kurapika letters

Melody thinks of the letter intended for Halkenburg and describes it as containing information about Benjamin’s secrets, Tserriednich’s depravity, the truth of Halkenburg’s birth, and information that could shake the succession contest’s foundations. She hopes Halkenburg can use it.

Kurapika is shown reading a letter, and Melody hopes he can devise a way to end the contest without further prince deaths. Chapter 402 does not show Kurapika’s response or solution and does not establish that the contest can already be stopped.
