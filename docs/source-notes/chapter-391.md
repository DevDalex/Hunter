# Chapter 391 source boundary

Reviewed: 2026-08-09

## Source policy

The sole story source for this maintained Chapter 391 integration is the user-supplied Hunterpedia Chapter 391 synopsis. No chapter title was supplied, so no title is invented. Outside story claims and later-chapter outcomes are excluded from the Chapter 391 knowledge boundary.

## Chronology

- Chapter 391 opens directly on the Tier 3 panic caused by the two soldiers Hinrigh killed at the end of Chapter 390.
- The integration therefore retains **Voyage Day 10** continuity from that immediate handoff.
- The supplied synopsis gives no exact clock time. No clock time is invented.

## Xi-Yu search split

Hinrigh leaves Lynch and Zakuro to continue the Hisoka search while he separately traces Heil-Ly personnel.

He explicitly tells Lynch and Zakuro to **contact him before doing anything** if they find Hisoka. This is stored as a field-command protocol, not as a broader permanent chain-of-command rule beyond what the scene establishes.

## Bloody Mary expansion

Chapter 391 adds a second directly demonstrated Bloody Mary application:

- Zakuro deploys numerous drops of his blood;
- the drops move through the corridor while searching for Hisoka;
- Zakuro says they will run out of Nen in approximately **30 to 40 minutes**;
- after that, they return to ordinary blood.

Zakuro personifies the drops as each doing its best to find Hisoka. The archive records demonstrated search behavior but does **not** infer independent intelligence, exact sensory organs, search radius, target-lock rules, communication mechanics, or a universal blood-drop count.

Lynch says she can punch suspicious people found by the drops and question them, but Chapter 391 does not show another Body and Soul activation. No new Body and Soul mechanic is inferred from that plan.

## Hinrigh surveillance and inference boundary

Hinrigh works with a soldier and civilian camera footage to trace the female Heil-Ly member. He concludes that Heil-Ly is watching the hallway leading toward the standard cabins because it is an access route.

He states that:

- Morena’s face is known;
- Heil-Ly members can be distinguished by their aura;
- he wants to identify **all 23** as soon as possible.

While watching the route, Hinrigh wonders whether the Second-Track Fakers are preparing a rebellion after years of oppression and whether the current killing spree is training for that future move.

Those rebellion/training conclusions remain **Hinrigh’s hypotheses**, not confirmed Heil-Ly motives.

The synopsis also has Hinrigh think about Heil-Ly after its benefactor, Tserriednich, has cut ties with the group. The integration preserves that immediate operational context without adding unstated details about when, how, or why the break occurred.

## Biohazard naming and mechanics

Chapter 391 supplies the formal ability name **Biohazard** for the transformation ability that Chapter 390 could only label descriptively.

The Chapter 391 demonstrations add:

- a camcorder left in record mode is transformed into a small cat and used for surveillance;
- ordinary handcuffs are transformed into pigeons;
- a pigeon can reach a target and return to handcuff form around a limb;
- multiple cuffs can be used to restrain different limbs;
- Hinrigh states that the handcuff-pigeons are reinforced with aura;
- standard handgun bullets fail to stop those demonstrated pigeons.

Across Chapters 390–391, specific transformed objects preserve useful aspects of their original function: guns still fire, the camcorder remains useful for recording, and handcuffs can revert to restraints. The archive does **not** turn those examples into an unrestricted universal law that every transformed object preserves every original property.

The synopsis still does not supply Hinrigh’s Nen category, maximum transformed mass, simultaneous transformation count, aura cost, total duration, animal-selection rule, or complete command range.

## Heil-Ly trio profile disclosure

Chapter 391 explicitly identifies:

- **Tevelares**: level 24, **Enhancer**, civil engineer;
- **Quorolle**: level 22, **Emitter**, repairman;
- **Padaille**: level 29, **Conjurer**, demolition worker.

These values are stored as Chapter 391 profile disclosures rather than backdated to earlier boundaries.

## Contagion +10 Nen-user rule

The trio explicitly treats killing a Nen user as worth **ten levels**.

However, they disagree about how that reward should be divided when several members contribute:

- Padaille argues the killing blow should receive all ten;
- the others discuss sharing;
- they expect Morena, as the ability user and “game master,” to decide the details.

Because they disagree, Chapter 391 does **not** establish the exact multi-attacker allocation rule.

Quorolle further speculates that Morena is probably able to tell what the members are doing at all times. This remains **Quorolle’s inference**, not a demonstrated Contagion surveillance mechanic.

## Fistful of Weapons

**Fistful of Weapons** is explicitly named as Padaille’s ability, and Padaille is explicitly identified as a Conjurer.

The chapter directly demonstrates Padaille transforming his right hand into:

- a hammer;
- a drill;
- an axe.

The drill is used to slip a handcuff and attack Hinrigh. The axe is used in an attempt to free himself from restraint.

Padaille’s internal desire to be “reborn as a weapon” is stored as characterization and motivation, not as a literal reincarnation mechanic.

The synopsis does not establish an exhaustive weapon list, whether only the right hand can transform, duration, switching limit, aura cost, durability rules, or any additional forms.

## Padaille death boundary

Hinrigh eventually forces Padaille’s own axe-form hand into the back of his head, killing him.

Tevelares and Quorolle escape and decide to ask Morena for instructions rather than risk capture and torture over the hideout location.

Padaille’s body death is therefore confirmed at the Chapter 391 boundary.

## Misha boundary

After killing Padaille, Hinrigh says that he is now **counting on Misha**.

The supplied synopsis does not explain what Hinrigh expects Misha Hao to do, what contingency she represents, or what ability she may have. The archive records only the named reliance and leaves the task unresolved.

## Corrected pre-existing range leakage

Before this integration, a broad legacy Heil-Ly/Xi-Yu state treated Chapter 391 as part of a 391–398 summary and mixed later hidden-route and public-violence developments into the same range. Chapter 391 now has its own exact organization state. The broader post-391 state begins later, so Padaille’s fight, the +10 Nen-user discussion, Biohazard’s named reveal, and the trio’s profile data stay anchored to the chapter where they are actually supplied.
