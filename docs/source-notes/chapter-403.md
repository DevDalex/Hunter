# Chapter 403 source note

## Source boundary

The modern Chapter 403 packet uses the current user-supplied Hunterpedia-style synopsis provided on 2026-08-10 as its sole substantive story source. No Chapter 403 title is supplied in the current synopsis, so no title or Japanese title is invented. The pre-existing title catalog is not used to backfill the chapter title. No Chapter 404+ story claim is imported to complete a Chapter 403 uncertainty.

Chapter 403 is present-day **Voyage Day 11, Wednesday**. The exact supplied clock anchors are **6:45 a.m.** for Balsamilco's arrival at the Tier 2 Ministry of Justice Prosecution and Courthouse Area entrance and **7:50 a.m.** for Benjamin's later red-alert briefing to Butch. Other scenes are kept in chapter presentation order without invented intermediate times.

## Balsamilco courthouse approach / TSK-17 boundary

At 6:45 a.m., Balsamilco reaches the Tier 2 Justice prosecution/courthouse entrance and passes the security scans. He reviews Halkenburg's guard distribution and believes the prince lacks his closest men and therefore should be unable to reproduce the powerful rumbling.

Balsamilco also reviews the intended courtroom attack. He expects his proximity to Halkenburg during cross-examination to make the pathological agent fatal to the target while leaving more distant judges and lawyers at much lower risk. He regards a wider outbreak as unlikely because the courtroom is closed to the public and thinks martial law could suppress a larger emergency if necessary.

Chapter 403 supplies the weapon designation **TSK-17** through Benjamin's later analysis. The archive records that name and the fact that Balsamilco intends to deploy the already-prepared Chapter 402 agent. It does not invent additional manufacturing, dosing, dispersal, or containment instructions beyond the story synopsis.

Most importantly, the original Balsamilco has **not completed the planned infection before Halkenburg's arrow operation reaches him**. The chapter therefore does not attribute Halkenburg's initial collapse to a successful pre-arrow TSK-17 exposure.

## Vict / rumbling boundary

In a narrow Justice corridor, Balsamilco senses someone behind him and sees Vict. Rumbling begins even though Balsamilco believed Halkenburg lacked the necessary guard group. Vict communicates in military sign language that he discovered the secret of Halkenburg's ability.

Balsamilco wonders whether Vict is unable or prevented from speaking. That is Balsamilco's question, not a confirmed diagnosis. Chapter 403 does not identify Vict's controlling consciousness, establish that Vict is manipulated, or explain why he communicates only by sign in this scene.

## The Boy Who Shoots the Arrow: Grimmel the Dissonance

Halkenburg is shown with **twelve civilian followers**. A man seated with a laptop covertly communicates Balsamilco's distance using his fingers while the laptop screen hides the signals from Balsamilco. Halkenburg adjusts his position and aims through the wall toward Balsamilco.

The synopsis supplies the official ability name **The Boy Who Shoots the Arrow: Grimmel the Dissonance**. Halkenburg fires the collective aura arrow, and the later reveal establishes that Halkenburg's soul/consciousness successfully occupies Balsamilco's body.

This confirms that the collective attack can be powered by loyal civilian supporters rather than only Halkenburg's personal royal guards. It also establishes a successful Balsamilco possession at this chapter boundary. The archive does not invent a universal wall-penetration rule, exact maximum range, exact supporter minimum, exact aura contribution per participant, or the location/state of Balsamilco's displaced consciousness.

## Benjamin's first analysis

Benjamin learns by phone that the Balsamilco-Halkenburg proceeding has not begun because both sides asked for a schedule change. Balsamilco's equipment-related request was rejected, while Halkenburg's illness was accepted and the proceeding was postponed until he recovered.

Benjamin reasons from the earlier rumbling that Halkenburg's ability was probably directed at Balsamilco. He considers and rejects an immediate TSK-17 explanation for Halkenburg's symptoms because, in his understanding, the timing would be too early. He also struggles with the fact that he believed Halkenburg could not use the ability alone.

When Benjamin learns that roughly ten supporters rushed to Halkenburg after his collapse and that some remain with him, he concludes that organizational assignment may be irrelevant if sufficiently loyal supporters can participate. The observed Chapter 403 arrow scene independently supports the broader point that civilians can contribute, but Benjamin's exact theory about loyalty thresholds remains his interpretation.

## Benjamin's manipulation hypothesis

While waiting for Balsamilco to come to the phone, Benjamin suspects that Balsamilco was hit and is no longer speaking under his own will. He therefore hypothesizes that Halkenburg's ability is a coercive Manipulation ability and revisits Balsamilco's earlier statement that manipulation is "first come, first served."

Benjamin wonders whether an unusually powerful collective Nen effect can defeat or bypass that ordinary assumption. Chapter 403 confirms Balsamilco's body is controlled by Halkenburg, but it does **not** independently settle a formal Nen category for the ability or establish a universal exception to first-come-first-served manipulation.

## Balsamilco phone deception / Benjamin's contingency

The Balsamilco body tells Benjamin that the mission has not yet been executed, that Halkenburg's arrow missed, and that Halkenburg may have collapsed from repeated use or from relying on civilians. These statements are made after the successful possession and therefore cannot be treated as reliable testimony from the original Balsamilco.

Benjamin continues to suspect a trap. He treats a return to Tier 1 without completing the anti-Halkenburg mission as possible collusion/coup behavior and considers declaring martial law and purging both men in that scenario. When the Balsamilco body asks permission to pursue Halkenburg in the sick bay, Benjamin leaves the tactical choice to him and demands results. The Balsamilco body promises results within the day.

Special Martial Law is **not yet declared or active** in Chapter 403. Benjamin has moved from Chapter 402's statement that current conditions did not qualify to an explicit conditional contingency tied to Balsamilco's suspicious movement or failure.

## 7:50 a.m. red alert

At 7:50 a.m., Benjamin decides to keep suspecting Balsamilco until the mission can be independently confirmed. He tells Butch that the rumbling was an attack on Balsamilco, orders red alert, and says he will declare martial law if Balsamilco leaves the Justice Bureau without notification. Butch is ordered to alert Tiers 2 and 3.

Benjamin then allows Kaiser and Fugetsu to approach because he sees value in maintaining workable relations with people he may need to govern alongside if he becomes king.

The red-alert order is confirmed. The martial-law declaration remains conditional and has not occurred at the Chapter 403 boundary.

## Unma / Halkenburg biological parentage

In Room 01, First Queen Unma speaks by phone. After several partially unspecified confirmations and reassurances, she addresses **Ninth Prince Halkenburg as her son**, tells him she is proud of him, and expresses regret that she could not see him at the banquet.

This scene directly establishes Unma as Halkenburg's biological mother in the supplied Chapter 403 synopsis.

The later Kacho letter says that Unma is Halkenburg's real mother and recounts a prevailing online theory that Unma asked Duazul to raise him in order to protect him from assassination by older siblings. Benjamin dismisses the letter as online rumor/speculation. The archive therefore separates the confirmed maternity from the **unconfirmed reason for the transfer to Duazul's household** and from any claim that Unma is secretly directing Halkenburg's current operation.

## Kacho letter / Benjamin and Kaiser boundary

Benjamin reads Kacho's letter, dismisses its political value, and sees the Unma-Duazul explanation as speculation that could already be found online. He judges future letters unlikely to contain intelligence beyond his own reach.

Kaiser had hoped to unsettle Benjamin but deliberately avoided material that could make Benjamin suspect the Ministry of Justice as the source. Kaiser nevertheless concludes that the audience was useful because Benjamin's willingness to invoke martial law appears to be increasing.

Benjamin's statements and Kaiser's observation are recorded separately. Kaiser's assessment of the probability of martial law is analysis, not a declaration.

## Letter-delivery status / Fugetsu condition

Zhang Lei, Camilla, Tubeppa, and Tyson are shown reading their letters. Tserriednich and Marayam reject the letters, and Halkenburg remains under medical treatment.

In Kaiser's office, Kacho-form says the operation still succeeded in an important respect because Luzurus's room has been marked/visited for the planned Magical Worm route. Fugetsu sleeps deeply in the infirmary. Melody says Basho's help continues to keep the hostile spirits away even while Fugetsu is alone and recommends rest so Fugetsu can recover enough strength to use her ability when needed.

Kacho-form declares that the Luzurus operation will be carried out **the night of the following day**. Chapter 403 does not show that operation occurring and does not confirm Luzurus as the culprit.

## Kurapika / succession ritual boundary

In Room 1014, Kurapika tells Oito that death awaits a participant who tries to leave the ship during the succession and explains that the Black Whale itself has become the **urn for the worm toxin**. Oito interprets this as requiring the princes to continue until only one survives.

Kurapika responds that the powerful Nen surrounding the ship and the no-escape condition may look as though only death is permitted, but he sees a possible opening and asks Oito for permission to act to protect Woble and the other young princes.

The archive records Kurapika's explanation and his belief that a route may exist. It does not claim Chapter 403 reveals the complete ritual mechanism, a proven method for ending the contest, or a demonstrated escape route.

## Zhang Lei coin progression

In Room 1003, Zhang Lei examines seven coins currently in his possession. He states that three additional coins were given away, for ten produced coins in total, and that one coin has changed from **1 to 10** that day.

Zhang Lei and his guards discuss several hypotheses. One suggests that because one coin is produced per day, the value change may occur after ten days. Zhang Lei decides to mark the higher-value coin with the date from ten days earlier and date subsequent coins so the hypothesis can be tested within at most ten days. Another guard suggests following up on distributed coins, which Zhang Lei accepts.

Other suggestions about holder value, growth parameters, monetary appreciation, or exponential value increases remain guesses. Zhang Lei regards the ideas as limited because his guards lack Nen expertise and concludes he needs Kurapika's help, ordering contact with Room 1014.

Chapter 403 therefore confirms the **observed 1 → 10 value change**, the ten-produced/three-distributed accounting supplied by Zhang Lei, and the planned date-tracking test. It does not confirm the ten-day rule, monetary value, growth parameter, exponential progression, holder effect, or final ability.

## Halkenburg mass-syncope / Worio Bay

Kaiser and Steiner discuss prior notice from Halkenburg that visitors might experience a mass-hypnosis-like fainting effect and quickly recover. Justice has indeed received a report that roughly a dozen supporters simultaneously fainted after meeting Halkenburg and then recovered.

Steiner adds that a Justice staff member experienced similar symptoms at a different time and place. Kaiser decides to interview the staff member, **Worio Bay**, in Room E-6 because he hopes the anomaly may reveal Halkenburg's intentions without risking a direct meeting while the prince's ability remains unknown.

Halkenburg's public explanation that the episodes come from joy and released anxiety is preserved as his stated cover explanation, not a confirmed medical or Nen mechanism.

## Kaiser / Worio disclosure

Kaiser tells Worio that supernatural abilities are real, that princes are using them in the succession, and that Kacho died during an escape attempt blocked by a shipwide barrier connected in some way to Nasubi. Kaiser also states that, apart from his Justice duties, he supports Halkenburg as long as Fugetsu's safety is assured.

Worio then displays a feather/pinion mark on the back of his left hand and says Halkenburg instructed him to explain everything if Kaiser independently raised the subject of Nen. Worio says Halkenburg expected Kaiser to connect the mass-fainting incidents and that Kaiser's avoidance of a direct meeting itself demonstrates his Nen awareness.

Worio then tells Kaiser that **Halkenburg is going to die soon**. This is a direct warning from Worio. Chapter 403 does not yet show the original Halkenburg body dead or establish the exact timing/cause/consequence of that expected death.

## Halkenburg inside Balsamilco

The chapter finally reveals the deception directly: while Benjamin hears the Balsamilco body report that the mission has been completed and that results will be delivered that night, **Halkenburg's soul/consciousness is occupying Balsamilco's body**.

Halkenburg's original body lies unconscious with an IV while the twelve supporters who powered the arrow surround him. Kaiser enters and observes from a distance.

The archive treats Halkenburg-in-Balsamilco as confirmed at Chapter 403. Balsamilco's displaced consciousness remains unresolved. The original Halkenburg body remains alive/unconscious at this boundary despite Worio's warning that death is approaching. No Chapter 404+ death/funeral consequence is imported.

## Kurapika's public-letter strategy

Back in Room 1014, Oito asks whether Kurapika wants to make a letter public. Kurapika clarifies that he means **the letter addressed to Oito**, not a letter addressed to himself. Oito gives permission.

Kurapika gives three strategic reasons: publication may reduce dangerous suspicion among higher-ranked princes whose letters expose scandals or crimes; Kacho's wishes may dampen the contest; and advertising publication during the next Nen lessons may provoke contact from higher-ranked princes. He also recognizes that there may be no useful response.

Kurapika intends to use postponing or canceling publication as a bargaining chip depending on who contacts Room 1014 and why. The second round of Nen lessons is scheduled to begin the next day, with attendance again left to the princes.

The contents of Oito's letter are not supplied in the current synopsis and are not invented.

## Zhang Lei meeting endpoint

Later, Kurapika and Oito go to Room 1003 and are welcomed by Zhang Lei's guards. Chapter 403 ends at the beginning of this contact. The archive does not import the Chapter 404 discussion, coin experiment, or any later result into this boundary.
