## Parallel Future / Laplace's Devil findings

The supplied synopsis names Tserriednich's technique **Ephemeral Ten Seconds: Laplace's Devil** while the maintained archive continues using the stable ability entity **Parallel Future**. Treat the chapter name as an alias/expanded technique label rather than creating a duplicate ability entity.

Chapter 419 materially upgrades several Chapter 418 hypotheses into demonstrated operational rules:

- Tserriednich estimates the sustained influence radius at **approximately 36 meters** from the activation point after observing where Room 1006 lookouts begin seeing the real him while Room 1004 lookouts remain locked into the imposed future;
- worsening static/buzzing correlates with approaching the edge of that influence;
- crossing the field boundary can **force the sustained effect to deactivate without Tserriednich releasing Zetsu**;
- Tserriednich believes he can retrigger Ephemeral Ten Seconds at or after the boundary so long as Zetsu remains maintained;
- observers who were **outside the influence when the effect was activated do not become affected merely by entering the sphere later**;
- affected observers can continue perceiving predicted gunfire, wounds, deaths, and a false Tserriednich body while the real Tserriednich takes a different route;
- when the sustained effect ends, the false body can disappear from affected observers' perception and people previously perceived as shot dead can be found alive.

The radius remains an **approximate operational measurement**, not an independently surveyed geometric constant. The exact shape of the field, exact centimeter/meter boundary, maximum aura duration, recharge ratio, and every possible entry/exit edge case remain unresolved.

## Room 1004 / Room 1006 boundary test

Tserriednich walks from Room 1004 toward the shared VVIP corridor while the two Room 1004 soldiers remain unable to see him. Soldiers near Room 1006 do see the disguised figure but choose not to interfere because the Room 1004 lookouts show no reaction.

This is the first direct field-boundary comparison in the maintained material and supports Tserriednich's approximately 36-meter estimate.

## Shared VVIP corridor combat

The sustained effect breaks as Tserriednich enters the soldier-filled shared VVIP corridor. He immediately uses handguns, takes a soldier hostage, and then activates Ephemeral Ten Seconds: Laplace's Devil again.

The future vision shows a costly firefight in which he would expend ammunition, suffer a right-leg gunshot, and draw another weapon. Instead of following that future, he runs left and uses the imposed perception to make multiple soldiers believe they were shot while he reaches the corner.

Do not collapse the predicted firefight and the real route into one physical sequence. The archive must preserve the distinction between **future shown / future perceived** and **Tserriednich's actual movement**.

## Initial-membership experiment

After rounding the corner, Tserriednich identifies soldiers who appear affected and others arriving from outside the original sphere. He deliberately calls outside soldiers toward him.

One entrant continues to see and respond to the real disguised Tserriednich after entering the sphere. This demonstrates that **being inside the radius later is insufficient**: an observer who was outside at activation remains outside the imposed future-perception set for that activation.

This finding resolves the central Chapter 418 question about a person entering the field after activation, while leaving other activation-membership edge cases open.

## Forced deactivation and Royal Army interpretation

Tserriednich deliberately exits the ability radius while maintaining Zetsu, forcing the sustained effect to end. The apparent corpse disappears as soldiers try to identify it, and soldiers previously believed dead are found alive.

The Royal Army cannot explain the contradictory observations. After soldiers in connected corridors compare matching impossible gunfire/corpse scenes, they initially suspect advanced technology and call for **gas masks and thermal scopes**.

The chapter does not establish that the military understands Nen or correctly identifies Tserriednich's ability.

## Escape-route update

Chapter 418's preferred **Route A** plan is superseded by circumstances rather than shown succeeding.

Tserriednich observes the Royal Army prioritizing King Nasubi's safety and decides to exploit the disruption by moving toward a **Tier 2 connecting passage below the entertainment facilities**, planning to traverse the VVIP entertainment hall and use Laplace's Devil against soldiers along the route if needed.

He notices that the bridge connecting Tier 1 to the lower tiers has been retracted from the lower-tier side and infers that the military intends to confine passengers for a sweep. He further infers that three key areas are already sealed: the princes' living quarters, the King/Queens' living area, and the VVIP area.

These are Tserriednich's tactical observations/inferences. Do not convert them into a complete omniscient map of the entire martial-law lockdown.
