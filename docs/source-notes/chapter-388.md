# Chapter 388 source note

## Source boundary

The sole story source for this integration is the user-supplied Hunterpedia Chapter 388 synopsis and its supplied note text. No outside story source is used to fill omissions. No chapter title was supplied, so none is invented.

## Chronology

- The Room 1014 class material continues the established Voyage Day 9 Nen-class sequence.
- Intermediate post-class scenes remain in publication sequence without an invented clock time.
- The chapter supplies an exact endpoint: Voyage Day 10 at **11:30 a.m.**, when Kurapika, Bill, and Oito feel the fourth aura rumbling.

## Nen-class boundaries

- Ladiolus, Maor, Yuri, and Satobi are the four students awakened that day. Satobi’s inclusion comes from the supplied Chapter 388 note explaining the obscured Viz panel.
- Furykov’s comparison of Ladiolus’s aura to roughly one year of three-hours-per-day basic practice is his estimate, not a literal training history.
- Furykov’s judgment that Ladiolus and Maor do not appear manipulated is an observer assessment, not a universal manipulation-detection rule.
- Kurapika’s gag order suppresses individual test results until everyone has tested. The archive does not invent the students’ Nen categories.

## Bill and Stealth Dolphin

- Kurapika explicitly identifies Bill as an Enhancer.
- Bill directly demonstrates an ability that causes growth of its target: the water overflows and a seed sprouts.
- The supplied text gives no official name for Bill’s ability. The archive label **Bill’s Growth Ability** is descriptive only.
- No unsupported range, cost, cooldown, maximum growth, healing, regeneration, aging, or general biological rule is inferred.
- Kurapika borrows Bill’s ability and lends it as part of the semi-coercive awakening procedure. Yuri is explicitly shown receiving the borrowed ability through Stealth Dolphin.
- Kurapika tells Yuri that the aura is borrowed and the ability is not her own. The archive does not generalize this one procedure into a claim that every transferable ability awakens Nen identically.

## Character-hypothesis boundaries

The following remain character theories or strategic estimates rather than confirmed mechanics:

- Babimyna’s suspicion that Kurapika may possess multiple abilities, perhaps one for each finger of his right hand.
- Rihan’s concern that Kurapika could have five abilities and therefore be a difficult Predator target.
- Rihan’s thought that Tubeppa’s unseen Guardian Spirit Beast might be especially dangerous.
- Balsamilco’s speculation about what kind of ability could have caused Shikaku’s suicide, including the rejected self-sacrifice possibility.
- Tubeppa’s inference from Salé-salé’s banquet absence and her conclusion that Benjamin’s camp may target her next.
- Tubeppa’s hope that giving recently awakened Heisen a mission might influence the ability he develops.

## Alliance boundary

Tubeppa authorizes Maor and Longhi to continue negotiations with Woble’s camp and permits more guards to attend future Nen classes. This is stored as a Chapter 388 **negotiation-stage** relationship. Later formal alliance terms are not backdated.

## Halkenburg-thread boundary

The chapter closes with the fourth aura rumbling and explicitly notes shorter intervals. The archive preserves the previously established connection to the Halkenburg thread but does not invent the immediate target, attacker, trigger, or complete mechanism of the fourth pulse from Chapter 388 alone.
