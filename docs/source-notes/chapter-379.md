# Chapter 379 source notes

Source basis: user-supplied Hunterpedia Chapter 379 synopsis and chapter notes only.

## Chronology

The supplied Chapter 379 material does not provide a new voyage-day timestamp. The maintained archive keeps Chapter 379 on Voyage Day 4 as immediate continuity from Chapter 378, but labels that day placement as inferred rather than explicitly timestamped.

## Cashew testimony

Cashew's interview statements cannot be treated wholesale as factual observations. Chapter 379 reveals that she is a Heil-Ly accomplice deliberately mixing truths and lies according to a plan with Luini. Her disclosure of Luini's crescent scar and clues about his transportation ability is intentional counterintelligence designed to test the Royal Army and Hunter Association investigation.

## Luini transportation ability

Chapter 379 explicitly establishes the following Luini-specific mechanics:

- the origin must be a sealed room;
- that room must have exactly one door;
- Luini can travel through the walls of that room to locations he has marked;
- he can return to the origin while the door remains closed;
- opening the only door breaks the sealed condition and resets the setup.

The source does not provide an official ability name, maximum range, maximum number of marks, complete marking procedure, or every transport limitation. Phinks discusses requirements of spatial transportation abilities, but the supplied synopsis does not reproduce enough of that explanation to promote it into a universal rule for all teleportation Nen.

## Luini level state

Chapter 378 stated that Luini gained twenty-one levels through twenty Tier 3 civilian kills and one Cha-R warehouse-guard kill. Chapter 379 explicitly states that three additional Cha-R guard kills bring Luini to level 24. The maintained archive therefore records level 24 as confirmed at the Chapter 379 boundary.

## Cha-R / Phantom Troupe cooperation

Ken'i Wang publicly proposes cooperation with Nobunaga, Phinks, and Feitan after clearing them of the warehouse attack. His private thoughts simultaneously conclude that the Phantom Troupe is dangerous enough to be crushed. The archive therefore records tactical cooperation under concealed strategic hostility, not friendship or stable trust.
