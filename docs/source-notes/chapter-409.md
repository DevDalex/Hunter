## Special Martial Law boundary

The chapter repeats that Special Martial Law is active and is not a drill, then broadcasts tier-specific instructions:

- **Tier 5:** follow military-police and boss instructions, remain calm, and understand that the reported problem is in an upper tier.
- **Tier 4:** the passage closure is described as preventing an upper-tier problem from spreading downward; safety measures are said to be in place.
- **Tier 3:** face the nearest wall, kneel, put both hands on the wall, remain still, and obey the royal army; noncompliance is threatened with gunfire.
- **Tier 2:** return to rooms, hold nothing, leave doors open, and keep hands visibly positioned while awaiting further orders.
- **Tier 1:** return to rooms and await a military investigation until ordinary activity is restored.

Borksen interprets Special Martial Law through her military background as granting the royal army broad authority to shoot suspicious people without warning, disarm civilians, and eliminate subversives. The archive preserves that as **Borksen’s trained interpretation**, not as a separately sourced legal-code quotation.

The supplied synopsis does **not** identify the incident or actor that caused Special Martial Law. That trigger remains unresolved at the Chapter 409 boundary.

## Deal / kiss / joining-condition boundary

Borksen selects **Deal**. Morena says Deal lets Borksen recover a response card of her choice from the graveyard if she fulfills Morena’s preselected “small request.” The request is a prolonged mouth-to-mouth kiss. Morena then explains that the kiss is not merely personal: it is also connected to the conditions under which she infects recruits with her ability.

Morena gives **three conditions** for Borksen to count as having joined Heil-Ly:

1. the negotiation game ends with **Yes**;
2. Morena infects Borksen through a kiss;
3. Borksen is present where Morena or another Heil-Ly member commits murder.

Morena says the order does not matter, but **all three conditions are required**.

Borksen agrees to the Deal request and the kiss occurs. This satisfies the kiss condition as described by Morena. However, Chapter 409 does **not** show Borksen receiving a Nen ability, level, or completed awakening, and the supplied synopsis does **not** show the required murder-presence condition being fulfilled. The archive therefore does **not** mark Borksen as a completed Heil-Ly member at the Chapter 409 endpoint.

## X recovery / card-integrity boundary

After the Deal request, Morena returns **X** from the graveyard. Before Orarge shuffles again, Borksen stops him because she took her eyes off the two untouched face-down cards during the kiss. Borksen insists on checking them despite Morena’s complaints about distrust and privacy.

The two untouched cards are directly verified as:

- **No**
- **Return**

Orarge then shuffles **X, No, and Return**. Borksen draws **X** again, removing it from play once more.

This sequence is strong evidence about the observed cards in this specific game. It does **not** establish a universal supernatural anti-cheating mechanism.

## Question A / hideout-location boundary

Borksen selects **Question A** and uses it to locate the hideout.

Morena answers:

- Tier 5? **No**
- Tier 4 or Tier 3? **No**
- Tier 1 or Tier 2? **No**
- On the Black Whale? **Yes**
- Made with a Nen ability? **Yes and No**

A loud rumble is then heard and felt as the central gate connecting Tiers 2 and 3 closes. Borksen asks whether the hideout is **between Tiers 2 and 3**, and Morena answers **Yes**.

This resolves the live spatial state: the Chapter 409 negotiation room is in an **inter-tier Heil-Ly hideout between Tiers 2 and 3**, not on one of the five ordinary tiers.

Borksen further infers that the arrangement must have been planned before the Black Whale was built and guesses the hideout is near the center of the inter-tier space. Those construction-history and exact-centrality conclusions remain **Borksen’s inference**, not confirmed narrator facts.

## Hideout entrances boundary

Asked whether an entrance is somewhere ordinary people can access, Morena answers **Yes and No**. She confirms multiple entrances, and after Borksen narrows the count, confirms **five doors**.

The archive therefore records:

- inter-tier location between Tiers 2 and 3: confirmed;
- aboard the Black Whale: confirmed;
- five entrances: confirmed;
- ordinary accessibility: qualified **Yes and No**;
- physical-versus-Nen construction: qualified **Yes and No**;
- exact five-door route map: not supplied.

## Heil-Ly headcount / Nen-type boundary

Under Question A, Morena confirms that the current Heil-Ly has **twenty-one members total**. She says:

- Morena is the **only Specialist** among the current group;
- there is **at least one Enhancer**;
- she does **not know all twenty other members’ abilities**.

The archive does not use this breakdown to identify the unnamed Chapter 408 smell-based category detector. That person remains unnamed unless a supplied chapter explicitly names them.

## Morena goal boundary

Near the end of Question A, Borksen tells Morena that she is glad they talked and asks whether Morena intends to change her goal. She repeats the question four times and receives **No** four times.

The archive records the repeated refusal and Borksen’s disappointment, but does not reinterpret Borksen’s later Yes choice as ideological agreement. Her motive remains unresolved.

## Final No / Return draw

Orarge confirms the final two response cards are **No** and **Return**, shuffles them, and lays them down.

Borksen sends one card to Morena and keeps the other. Morena agrees to Borksen’s proposal that they reveal them simultaneously. The result is:

- Morena: **No**
- Borksen: **Return**

Borksen visibly reacts with relief. Morena accepts the result and tells Borksen to exchange Return for the response card she wants restored, after which the game will end according to the restored response.

## Final Yes stopping point

Borksen deliberately exchanges **Return for Yes**. Morena is surprised and asks whether she made a mistake. Borksen explicitly confirms that she did **not**.

That is the strict Chapter 409 stopping point.
