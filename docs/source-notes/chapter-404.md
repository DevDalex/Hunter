# Chapter 404 source note

## Source boundary

The modern Chapter 404 packet uses the current user-supplied Hunterpedia-style synopsis provided on 2026-08-11 as its sole substantive story source. No Chapter 404 title or Japanese title is supplied, so neither is invented or backfilled from the older title catalog. No Chapter 405+ scene is imported to complete a Chapter 404 uncertainty.

Chapter 404 begins on **Voyage Day 11, Wednesday**, continues into the night, and reaches the **early hours of Voyage Day 12, Thursday** when Halkenburg's original body dies. The supplied scheduling anchors are the Guardian Coin production time of **1:00 p.m. each day**, the second Nen class at **9:00 a.m. Thursday**, and the announced funeral departure at **12:00 p.m. Thursday**. The chapter announces that future procession; it does not show the casket departing.

## Zhang Lei coin experiment

Kurapika tells Zhang Lei that the ten-day change hypothesis is mostly supported, not conclusively proven. He reasons that persistent conjured objects should face some production limit and that circulation may allow more coins to exist away from the beast or host.

Tenftory discovers that the front of his distributed coin matches Zhang Lei's retained coins while its reverse design differs. Zhang Lei infers that holder state matters, but no actual power or benefit is demonstrated merely from the changed design.

Zhang Lei gives Kurapika the coin currently showing 10. In Kurapika's hand, the coin changes within seconds to the distributed-holder design and its number becomes 1. When Kurapika returns it, the design reverts to Zhang Lei's retained-holder design while the number remains 1 rather than returning to 10.

These observed transformations are canonical at the Chapter 404 boundary. The archive does not turn them into a complete transfer, reset, multiplication, ownership, or activation rule.

## Kurapika's coin hypotheses

Kurapika considers whether accepting a coin could satisfy a pseudo-coercive Manipulation condition. He judges an immediate murder-level command unlikely from the aura accumulated over roughly ten days, but this is risk analysis rather than a demonstrated safety guarantee.

Kurapika describes the beast's power as a **possible cumulative Conjuration ability** suited to Zhang Lei's long-range temperament. He proposes that circulated coins may store Nen and eventually grant powers or open a holder's aura nodes. His six-month/one-quintillion projection, the possible **10^64** threshold associated with “unimaginable,” the future ability-awakening model, and the concealed pseudo-coercive loyalty theory are all hypotheses. Chapter 404 does not confirm the Nen category, threshold, timetable, granted ability, or Manipulation component.

Kurapika reasons that two coins should now show 10 if appearance day counts as day one and suspects that Coventoba concealed the first day's coin. The daily **1:00 p.m.** production time and Coventoba's presence can be checked, but the synopsis does not show the hidden coin, its current number/design, or a direct confrontation with Coventoba.

Zhang Lei gives Kurapika the new coin produced that day. Its future development and any effect on Kurapika remain unresolved.

## Zhang Lei / Kurapika political boundary

Zhang Lei accepts Kurapika's useful analysis and says it gives him more room to consider a future with his siblings. He remains status-conscious: internally, he frames Kurapika's information as payment for Woble's safety rather than a relationship between equals.

Kurapika recognizes Zhang Lei's increasingly sophisticated negotiation and withholds the possible Manipulation/loyalty interpretation. He also accepts that remaining aligned with Zhang Lei exposes Woble's camp to the balance among Zhang Lei/Xi-Yu, Tserriednich/Heil-Ly, and Luzurus/Cha-R.

Kurapika's scenario in which Benjamin exploits a mafia war, kills a benefactor prince, frames the death as a mafia attack, or targets Woble is strategic forecasting. No such false-flag attack, all-out mafia war, prince assassination, house arrest, or Special Martial Law declaration occurs in Chapter 404. Special Martial Law remains inactive.

Cheadle accompanies Halkenburg's original body with five assistants, including Leorio. She treats a possible head injury from the fall, orders a CHEM-7 blood panel and CT scan, requests a week of condition history from Halkenburg's Tier 1 guards, and sends the vomitus to Gel in forensics.

The autopsy refusal and final official classification are plans, not completed results at this boundary.

Halkenburg's original body subsequently dies after attempted resuscitation in the early hours of Thursday. The death is confirmed in Chapter 404. The supplied synopsis establishes the fatal operation and expected syndrome but does not supply additional weapon-handling detail.

## Grimmel the Dissonance mind-swap rules

Halkenburg explains the ability as a forced mind swap: one participant from his contributing group is selected at random and exchanges minds with the person struck by the arrow.

The Shikaku/Sumidori experiment confirms that only one of the exchanged minds can normally be awake at a time. The Halkenburg-side participant generally receives wake priority while both bodies remain available. After the Sumidori-controlled Shikaku body died, Sumidori's original body woke and gave Shikaku's service number, demonstrating that Shikaku's mind was then awake in Sumidori's body.

Halkenburg also states that the Vict experiment tested the opposite body-death order. If the body from Halkenburg's side dies first, the victim's mind returns to its original body, coexists there with the invading mind, and receives wake priority. The synopsis does not provide the complete Vict sequence, identify every involved consciousness by name, or turn this example into rules beyond Halkenburg's own explanation.

Halkenburg reasons that the succession condition requires both a target's body and mind to be dead. His answers to the two body-death scenarios and his language about sacrificial followers are recorded as the operator's explanation and strategy. The archive does not add a formal Nen category, a controllable participant-selection rule, or a metaphysical definition of death beyond what he states.

## Halkenburg / Balsamilco post-death plan

Halkenburg expects his original body's death to return Balsamilco's mind to Balsamilco's own body, where it will coexist with Halkenburg's invading mind and receive wake priority. He plans to time a sleeping pill immediately before the death so Balsamilco's reawakening is delayed, giving Halkenburg an estimated additional **ten-hour** operating window. Halkenburg says that timing can only be adjusted loosely.

After the original body dies, the Balsamilco body still speaks with Benjamin and Halkenburg internally thanks him, directly showing that Halkenburg remains operational through Balsamilco immediately after the death. The chapter does not show Balsamilco awake, identify the exact instant his mind returns, demonstrate the full ten-hour window, or show its endpoint.

At the Chapter 404 boundary, Halkenburg's original body is dead; Halkenburg's consciousness remains active in Balsamilco's living body; and Balsamilco's own mind is no longer treated as lost or dead, but it is not shown awake or in control.
