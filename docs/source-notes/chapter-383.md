# Chapter 383 source boundaries

Reviewed: 2026-08-09

Sole story source for the dedicated Chapter 383 upgrade: user-supplied Hunterpedia Chapter 383 synopsis and chapter notes.

## Title metadata

The supplied material states that Chapter 383 has the same title as Chapter 279, but it does not supply that title in English, Japanese, or romanized form. The dedicated Chapter 383 record therefore keeps the title fields null rather than importing or inventing a title.

## Chronology

Chapter 382 explicitly reaches **8:00 p.m. on Voyage Day 8** at the opening of the first Sunday banquet. Chapter 383 continues that same banquet and then the escape operation.

The synopsis mentions a point at which ninety minutes remain until the plan begins, but it does not provide enough clock information to assign an exact timestamp to that scene or to Melody’s later performance. Chapter 383 events are therefore recorded as **Voyage Day 8, after 8:00 p.m.** without inventing a more precise clock time.

## Salé-salé’s public absence

The banquet audience is told that Salé-salé cannot perform because of illness. Chapter 382 already established in the maintained chronology that Salé-salé was assassinated and that Yushohi reported the mission as successful.

The Chapter 383 archive therefore records **illness as the public banquet explanation**, not as Salé-salé’s actual medical state and not as a reversal of the Chapter 382 death record.

## Melody’s flute performance

Chapter 383 directly demonstrates that Melody can, by playing sincerely, entrance everyone who can hear her performance for **three minutes**. The listeners experience a vivid scenic vision, and the effect reaches people hearing the performance through the banquet loudspeakers, including princes who are not physically present in the hall.

Confirmed from the supplied synopsis:

- the effect is tied to hearing Melody’s performance;
- the demonstrated duration is three minutes;
- the effect incapacitates ordinary action strongly enough for Keeney and the twins to pass soldiers;
- relayed loudspeaker audio carries the effect in this demonstration;
- the listeners experience an expansive vision of mountains, flowers, and butterflies.

The supplied material does **not** provide an official ability name, formal Nen-type classification, maximum independent range, immunity rule, or repeat-use limit. The foundation therefore records the demonstrated mechanics while leaving classification and broader limits unresolved.

## Keeney’s death

The synopsis explicitly reveals Keeney’s earlier conversation with Melody: his wife and daughter died in an accident two years before the voyage, he says he has been searching for a place to die since then, and he also believes exposed accomplices could implicate the Hunter Association and damage its mission.

After Kacho and Fugetsu depart in the lifeboat, Keeney shoots himself and dies.

The archive preserves both parts of his stated reasoning without reducing the act to either a purely operational sacrifice or a purely private motive.

## Escape boundary and the hands

As the lifeboat approaches open water, a mass of hands surrounds and attacks Kacho and Fugetsu. Kacho concludes that a prince attempting to leave the Black Whale is driven toward death and orders Fugetsu to open a return door.

The archive records the **observed escape enforcement** and Kacho’s interpretation. It does not claim that Chapter 383 fully identifies:

- the exact Nen source of the hands;
- whether the hands are a Guardian Spirit Beast, a Seed Urn construct, or another ritual mechanism;
- the complete geographic boundary;
- every exception or enforcement condition of the ritual.

This chapter materially strengthens the rule that the succession process resists physical escape, but it does not finish the ritual’s full mechanical specification.

## Kacho’s death and the identity of the returning Kacho

This distinction is mandatory throughout the maintained archive.

**Human Kacho Hui Guo Rou dies during the failed escape.** Her original body is shown dead on the drifting lifeboat.

The Kacho who later opens the door, takes Fugetsu’s hand, returns with her, and lies beside her is **Without You**, Kacho’s Guardian Spirit Beast, which has taken Kacho’s form after her death.

The supplied Chapter 383 material does not establish that Kacho’s human consciousness survives inside Without You. The archive therefore keeps three concepts separate:

1. Kacho’s human body: deceased.
2. Kacho’s human consciousness after death: unconfirmed.
3. Without You in Kacho’s form: active Guardian Spirit Beast protection beside Fugetsu.

## Magical Worm

Chapter 383 names Fugetsu’s Guardian Spirit Beast ability **Magical Worm** and identifies it as a translocation tunnel/door system.

Confirmed in the supplied synopsis:

- the ability provides the twins’ translocation route;
- Fugetsu controls the outward journey;
- Kacho controls the return journey;
- Fugetsu creates the emergency return door during the failed lifeboat escape;
- Magical Worm is cooperative with Kacho’s Guardian Spirit Beast, Without You.

The synopsis does not fully define maximum range, destination-selection rules, reset conditions, or how the return-control role should be formalized after human Kacho’s death and the manifestation of Without You. Those remain open.

## Without You

Chapter 383 names Kacho’s previously formless Guardian Spirit Beast ability **Without You**.

Confirmed in the supplied synopsis:

- it activates when one of the twins dies;
- it can take the form of a twin;
- after Kacho dies, it takes Kacho’s form;
- its purpose is to protect the surviving sister;
- it remains beside the surviving sister until death;
- it operates cooperatively with Magical Worm.

The archive does not convert this protective imitation into proof of human resurrection or surviving human consciousness.

## Hunter Code retrospective

The Chapter 381 call from Mizaistom’s side outwardly instructed Melody to assist Keeney in preventing Kacho and Fugetsu from escaping. Chapter 383 reveals that this call carried a Hunter Code message with the opposite operational intent.

For the supplied official Viz translation, the opening time statement is **11:55** and the extraction produces:

**Assist Princes escape fully**

from the selected words in the following sentences.

The supplied notes state that the Japanese version instead uses **13:44** and an nth-clause method, while communicating the same hidden meaning.

This decode is stored as a **Chapter 383 retrospective reveal linked to Chapter 381**. It is not rewritten as explicit knowledge that the Chapter 381 archive had already established.

## Marayam’s isolated Room 1013

Chapter 383 adds a concrete spatial restriction: servants who leave the isolated Nen space in order to perform at the banquet are stated to be unable to return to it.

This produces an operational staffing change. Vergei accepts the one-way loss rather than risk a suspicious banquet withdrawal, while Biscuit proposes placing Hunters in the real Room 1013 and remaining inside the isolated space with Marayam.

The archive confirms the re-entry failure for the departing servants described in this chapter. It does not generalize the observation into a complete rule for every person, every type of exit, or every future state/reset of the Nen space.
