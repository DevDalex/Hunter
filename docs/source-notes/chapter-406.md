# Chapter 406 source note

## Source boundary

The modern Chapter 406 packet uses the current user-supplied Hunterpedia-style synopsis provided on 2026-08-11 as its sole substantive Chapter 406 story source. **No Chapter 406 title or Japanese title was supplied in the current packet.** Neither is invented or backfilled from the older Chapter 406 catalog. No Chapter 407+ scene is imported to complete a Chapter 406 uncertainty.

Chapter 406 continues directly from Chapter 405's Tajao-route cliffhanger and from the funeral timing established by the maintained Chapter 404–405 boundary. The supplied synopsis shows Halkenburg's funeral procession beginning on Tier 3. The archive therefore places the present sequence on **Voyage Day 12, Thursday, during the funeral-procession period**, without inventing an exact minute.

## Tajao route / outer structure boundary

Tajao continues guiding Nobunaga, Phinks, and Feitan through a cramped, winding corridor that runs through gaps between shipboard buildings. He warns Nobunaga that his visible sword may provoke young, inexperienced Tier 2 soldiers even though Tajao gave him an authorized ID.

The route opens into an enormous room filled with large pipes and a rickety staircase. Tajao identifies it as the outermost interior part of the Black Whale and says climbing the stairs reaches the top of Tier 2. This resolves Chapter 405's unseen-door destination without rewriting Chapter 405 as if the room had already been visible there.

The outer pipe/stair chamber is kept distinct from both the Chapter 405 **Heil-Ly processing area inside the Tier 2 hideout** and the separate **recyclable-waste/sewage processing plant between Tiers 4 and 5** described in Chapter 406.

## Waste-processing network / subcontractor theory boundary

Tajao states that waste from roughly 200,000 people is funneled through the ship and that the recyclable-waste and sewage plant is between Tiers 4 and 5. He says Cha-R and Xi-Yu control that processing area, making waste disposal convenient for the established mafia to control.

Nobunaga reasons that the families probably subcontract the actual work and leave routine oversight to underlings. Nobunaga, Phinks, and Feitan then theorize that Heil-Ly could pose as disposal contractors, summon victims to interviews one by one, kill them, and use the disposal system as an assembly-line murder pipeline.

That pipeline remains a **Troupe hypothesis**. Chapter 406 does not directly show Heil-Ly operating as a waste subcontractor, confirm a fake company, identify victims of such a system, or prove that the described plant is being used by Heil-Ly.

Nobunaga turns back because the theory gives him a reason to investigate. Phinks and Feitan continue the upward route with Tajao.

## Heil-Ly hideout defense recap boundary

Before separating, Nobunaga warns Phinks and Feitan about the counteractive defensive system at the Heil-Ly hideout: attacking the guard can trigger an invincibility-like defense, Nen security-doll guards can expel intruders, and the Room A/Room B door is a warp point to Tier 3.

The maintained archive already knows the Chapter 399 defense as Yokotani's **A Battle of Wits: "LSDF"** and the related hideout routing from earlier chapters. Chapter 406 is treated as Nobunaga's operational recap, not as a fresh demonstration of every mechanic.

Feitan proposes that the group might be safe if Heil-Ly attacks first and their response qualifies as self-defense. This remains Feitan's tactical theory. Chapter 406 does not demonstrate that self-defense bypasses LSDF or any other counteractive defense.

## Biohazard transmitter boundary

Inside the Heil-Ly hideout, the transmitter that Hinrigh had transformed into a raw oyster continues beeping and then reverts to its original transmitter form beneath the cabinet.

This is a new observed **Biohazard** reversion for the Chapter 398–399 tracking operation. The archive records the reversion without inventing the exact elapsed duration, exact remaining aura, a universal oyster-duration formula, or proof that the transmitter was discovered by Heil-Ly.

## Lynch recovery / knowledge-state boundary

Hinrigh and Zakuro move through the large Tier 3 funeral crowd. Lynch has not returned. A mafia member then takes them to Lynch's body, which soldiers found during a special patrol for Halkenburg's funeral procession.

The body shows a destroyed neck that was twisted and broken with speed and precision. At the global Chapter 406 canon boundary, Chapter 405 has already established that **Bonolenov killed Lynch**. Chapter 406 newly supplies the recovered body's physical condition. The archive does not rewrite Hinrigh or Zakuro's personal knowledge as if either man knows Bonolenov is the killer.

Hinrigh initially suspects a Heil-Ly hitman. After Zakuro recalls the post-encounter "Lynch" behaving unnaturally and distinctly insisting that the man was "definitely Hisoka," Hinrigh builds a different inference: the real Lynch was already dead, an impostor woke Zakuro, Lynch had discovered the supposed Hisoka was fake, and the deception was designed to make the mafia believe Hisoka had been found.

Hinrigh explicitly treats the reconstruction as assumptions and inferences. He does not identify Bonolenov, prove the transformation method, or learn the Chapter 405 reader-only decoy plan. Zakuro and Hinrigh nevertheless promise to avenge Lynch regardless of the culprit. Zakuro says a Phantom Troupe culprit could create conflict involving Cha-R, but the chapter does not begin such a war.

## Chrollo phone-search boundary

In the Tier 3 crowd, Chrollo opens Skill Hunter and uses a conjured-cell-phone search ability. The guidance first says the sought person is not in the current area and offers another-location/retry guidance while indicating that Chrollo has a limited number of calls remaining that day.

Chrollo considers trying from a different location or loosening the conditions. The archive preserves those thoughts without inventing an exact daily call count, signal radius, recharge rule, condition-editing formula, target-selection rule, or target identity.

The supplied trivia translates text visible on the Skill Hunter page as:

- "McGait Narumi";
- "Love Dial 6700 - Disgusting Telephone";
- a description saying a conjured cell phone searches for an ideal partner, criteria input produces a 6–20 digit number, and dialing that number provides guidance.

The archive records **Love Dial 6700 - Disgusting Telephone** as the supplied translated page name/label. It does **not** assume what "McGait Narumi" denotes, identify that text as the original user, or equate the ability's generic "ideal partner" wording with a confirmed identity for the person Chrollo is currently tracking.

## Sacred treasures / Skill Hunter evolution boundary

Chrollo identifies three Kakin sacred treasures:

- the **Seed Urn**, a ceremonial urn used by succession-contest participants;
- the **Lotus Anchorite**, a divine mummy that oversees the succession contest;
- the **Sword of Good Omens**, a succession sword passed to the next king.

Chrollo decides to search for the treasures in addition to Hisoka. He reasons from the army and mafia's unchanged movements that the treasures are unlikely to be on Tier 3 or below, then theorizes that the treasures are the core system creating the powerful Nen sustaining the succession contest and Kakin's prosperity and that Tier 1 is the only plausible place to keep them.

Those claims about the treasures' systemic Nen role, ritual focal points, security level, and Tier 1 storage remain **Chrollo's theories**. Chapter 406 does not directly show the treasures' current storage location, prove that they generate Kakin's prosperity, or show Chrollo stealing any of them.

Chrollo states a new condition relevant to his planned **Skill Hunter evolution**: before the ordinary conditions for stealing and storing the ability he wants, he must steal an item comparable to a national treasure. He believes the three sacred treasures can satisfy that prerequisite and enable the decisive Hisoka preparation.

Chapter 406 still does not identify the desired ability, show the evolution occurring, show a sacred treasure being stolen, or prove that the resulting preparation guarantees Hisoka's death.

## Spider continuity boundary

Chrollo says an alternative is already in place and that the Spider will continue even if he dies; the idea of the Spider, rather than Chrollo alone, is meant to remain the source of fear.

The existence of a continuity plan is direct. The chapter does not identify Chrollo's successor, define the alternative, establish a succession procedure, or prove what the Troupe would look like after his death.

## Funeral procession / final call boundary

The funeral procession begins. Halkenburg's guards carry his ornate casket while mourners bow and weep.

Chrollo makes another call. The search guidance says the person has been located but is outside the current Nen signal range and asks him to change coordinates. Chrollo concludes that the person is **above** his current Tier 3 position.

The archive records only that relative placement. The exact tier, room, identity, ability, and relationship of the tracked person remain unresolved.
