# Chapter 400 source note

## Source boundary

The modernized Chapter 400 story packet uses the **current user-supplied Hunterpedia Chapter 400 synopsis** as its sole substantive story source. The pre-existing maintained Chapter 400 packet is used only to retain already-verified chapter-title metadata: **Secrecy / 秘匿**. No outside website or later-chapter story claim is used to fill gaps in the supplied synopsis.

Chapter 400 is present-day **Voyage Day 10** material. No exact clock time is supplied or invented.

## Troupe / Heil-Ly tracking

Phinks and Feitan begin with the receiver reading the hidden transmitter at roughly **100–200 meters** with unstable direction, which Feitan recalls indicates a target directly above or below. Moving from Tier 3 toward Tier 4 makes the signal weaker. That observation confirms that the tracked transmitter and therefore the concealed Heil-Ly base are broadly **above Tier 3 on Tier 2**.

This is a **Chapter 400 location revelation**. Chapters 398–399 knew the Room 3101 access network and internal hideout rooms without the later Tier 2 physical fix. The archive therefore does not rewrite those earlier knowledge boundaries as though Tier 2 had already been established.

Tier 2 is only the broad physical level. Chapter 400 still does **not** establish an exact room number, complete footprint, full access topology, member-only route, or precise coordinates.

Nobunaga says that the enemy boss created the space with Nen and infers that the boss is in the upper tiers. That is preserved as **Nobunaga’s analysis**, not as omniscient confirmation that Morena personally created or operates the hidden Nen space, the Room 3101 teleport route, or the self-restoring hideout stage.

Phinks’s explanation of En is also personal. He says voices, movement inside his field, and his own movement interrupt his concentration; controlled warehouse searches are about his reliable use, and exposing En to a crowd of unknown Nen users would be strategically reckless. These are **Phinks-specific limitations**, not universal rules for En.

The Troupe continues to treat finding **Hisoka** as its primary objective. The Heil-Ly/mafia conflict remains secondary except where it affects upper-tier access and operational safety. Nobunaga proposes bringing Franklin, but Chapter 400 does not show Franklin joining this group.

The emergency broadcast is preserved only to the level supplied: it asks everyone to pay attention to sad and unfortunate news. The synopsis does not reproduce the substantive announcement, so no missing content is invented.

## Tyson / Izunavi boundary

Madwig, Himoncé, Anzel, Hyuga, and the additional attendant presented as a prince from planet Moony are performing personas corresponding to Tyson’s favorite roles. The archive does **not** treat those statements as literal evidence that the attendants are actually an amnesiac hitman, global celebrity, Tyson’s biological little brother, a legendary tutor, an extraterrestrial, or alien royalty.

Giuliano’s unusual calmness while reading Tyson’s book is observed. **Izunavi** suspects that this is connected to Tyson’s Guardian Spirit Beast and asks whether the book might be used to influence Nasubi and end the Succession Contest. That connection and strategic effect remain hypotheses. Tyson agrees to negotiate with her mother about a possible King-facing reading; Chapter 400 does not establish that Nasubi has read the book or that the book can end the contest.

## Kacho / Without You boundary

**Human Kacho remains dead.** Chapter 400 does not resurrect her.

The Kacho-form actor in the Justice Bureau scenes is **Without You**, Kacho’s death-triggered Guardian Spirit Beast. The Kacho-form actor reasons that it may have been resurrected as a guardian spirit, that Fugetsu being able to see it may mean Kacho is outside the contest, and that Fugetsu may be supplying aura to maintain the Kacho-form manifestation. Those statements are preserved as the actor’s own reasoning and are **not** promoted to settled metaphysical, contest-status, or aura-cost mechanics.

Human Kacho’s consciousness surviving inside Without You remains unresolved. Chapter 400 directly shows the Kacho-form actor moving through a bookcase / solid barrier while Kaiser uses an ordinary doorway; that traversal is recorded as demonstrated behavior without inventing a complete intangibility ruleset.

Seiko intentionally preserves the outside belief that Kacho remains alive. Her deception does not alter the archive’s actual life-state record for human Kacho.

## Melody / Kaiser boundary

Melody explicitly explains that her music is intended to **heal**, that the loss of consciousness observed during her banquet performance is a **side effect**, and that covering the ears prevents the demonstrated entrancement. These mechanics enter the archive at Chapter 400 and are not backfilled into earlier chapter-bounded knowledge.

Five princes seek Melody’s performance in different forms: Zhang Lei and Tserriednich request direct meetings / performances, while Benjamin, Tubeppa, and Luzurus use the language of a pardon and encore. Kaiser proposes exploiting Melody’s performance to incapacitate higher-ranking princes and administering slow-acting poison.

Kaiser also explicitly says he is in love with Melody. Melody hears a heartbeat she regards as unnaturally precise and privately theorizes that Kaiser may be a **Manipulator** or may be manipulating himself while presenting as a non-user. This remains **Melody’s suspicion**. Chapter 400 does not confirm Kaiser’s Nen type, a self-manipulation ability, an external controller, or the sincerity of his love declaration.

Kaiser explains the consequences of a possible future **Special Martial Law** declaration and prepares Steiner for a worst-case military takeover of the Justice Bureau. Special Martial Law is **not declared or active in Chapter 400**. Later declaration, enforcement, and consequences belong to later chapters and are not imported backward.

## Fugetsu / Magical Worm boundary

Fugetsu reports and demonstrates a major Chapter 400 change in **Magical Worm**: she can use the route multiple times rather than once per day, and a return door can appear while she is alone. The demonstrated solo-return restriction is that she returns to where she was.

Fugetsu also appears pale and exhausted, has heavy bags under her eyes, and eventually collapses. During a controlled hallway crossing, Melody senses Fugetsu’s aura as weak as Zetsu, hears an unstable heartbeat, and perceives numerous hostile / evil spirits around her. Melody concludes that an exorcist is urgently needed.

The archive does **not** assert that repeated Magical Worm use caused the deterioration, that Magical Worm automatically becomes stronger after every use, or that the hostile spirits are part of Magical Worm. The responsible user, official ability name, Nen category, activation route, ability body, motive, duration, and removal condition all remain unresolved. Melody’s theory that the condition may be a hostage-negotiation setup is an investigative hypothesis.

## Zhang Lei / Vantine interviews

Zhang Lei personally gives Melody a coin marked **1**. Vantine later appears on Tserriednich’s behalf. Melody intentionally introduces the word “negotiations” and hears confusion and doubt in Vantine’s heartbeat. Her resulting assessment that the visible Third- and Fourth-Prince camps are less likely to be the source of the suspected negotiation setup remains **Melody’s inference**, not an omniscient elimination of every possible connection.

## Longhi / Kurapika / Tubeppa boundary

Longhi tells Kurapika that Water Divination is unnecessary because she can already use Nen. After hearing Longhi’s explanation, Kurapika **accepts the contract** and explicitly agrees to collaborate with **Tubeppa**.

The supplied Chapter 400 synopsis does **not** reproduce the contract’s terms. Therefore the archive does not import **Moonlight Act**, later treaty mechanics, later penalties, later duration rules, or Chapter 401+ alliance consequences into the Chapter 400 boundary. At 400, the agreement exists and the terms remain unsupplied.

## Stopping point

At the Chapter 400 boundary:

- the tracked Heil-Ly base is broadly confirmed on **Tier 2**, but its exact coordinates and spatial-system creator remain unresolved;
- Hisoka remains the Phantom Troupe’s primary target;
- Tyson and Izunavi have an untested Book of Tyson / Nasubi plan;
- human Kacho remains dead while Without You continues in Kacho’s form;
- Melody’s hearing requirement, healing intent, and unconsciousness side effect are newly explicit;
- Kaiser’s assassination plan and love declaration are explicit, but his Nen status and real motive remain unresolved;
- Special Martial Law remains a future contingency only;
- Magical Worm has repeated-use and solo-return behavior, but the cause of that change is unknown;
- Fugetsu is alive but severely weakened and surrounded by an unidentified hostile-spirit condition;
- an exorcist is an urgent objective;
- Zhang Lei gives Melody a coin marked 1;
- Kurapika accepts Longhi’s contract and Tubeppa collaboration, while the contract terms remain unavailable;
- **no Chapter 401+ consequence or mechanic is backfilled.**
