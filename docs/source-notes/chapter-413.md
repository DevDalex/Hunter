# Chapter 413 source note

The **sole substantive story source** for this Chapter 413 modernization is the user-supplied Hunterpedia-style synopsis provided in chat on 2026-08-13. No Chapter 414+ story outcome is imported into the Chapter 413 boundary.

## Strict boundaries

- **Chronology:** Chapter 413 is a pre-declaration packet on Voyage Day 12. Halkenburg’s burial-chamber placement is stated to occur **thirty minutes before** the scheduled 2:15 p.m. Special Martial Law declaration, so the maintained archive records a derived 1:45 p.m. anchor. Benjamin orders preparatory operations for **2:00 p.m.**, fifteen minutes before the scheduled declaration. Chapter 413 schedules Special Martial Law but does **not** contain the later formal 2:15 p.m. declaration/enforcement maintained in Chapter 415.
- **Halkenburg body / soul:** Halkenburg’s **original body is dead** and is placed in the princes’ burial chamber. Halkenburg’s soul/consciousness remains active in **Balsamilco Might’s body**. The archive keeps body, identity, and consciousness separate instead of treating the corpse as revived.
- **Flame of Life / eligibility:** Nugui observes that Halkenburg’s associated **Flame of Life does not ignite**, unlike the Eighth, Tenth, and Twelfth Princes, and interprets that state as continued eligibility. Nasubi states that Halkenburg’s succession right remains while his soul resides in a body. These are preserved as the characters’ explicit observations/statements without inventing a complete burial-chamber rule system.
- **Halkenburg’s arrow:** Halkenburg begins another follower-powered activation, says continued Balsamilco impersonation is no longer practical, and tests a new hypothesis. He notes that an arrow has already been fired at Benjamin. The chapter does not resolve impact, another mind/soul exchange, or a new target state.
- **TSK-17:** Benjamin develops symptoms he identifies as TSK-17, checks the secure storage, and later receives Furykov’s explicit confession that **Furykov infected him**. Furykov’s deliberately identifiable delivery moment and account of coercion are preserved without adding an unshown technical delivery method.
- **Furykov / Beyond / Unma:** Furykov states that **Beyond Netero is his father**, shows the eye-shaped mark beneath his tongue, and says Beyond gave him a death-trigger curse that is practically impossible to remove. Furykov says Unma threatened to activate the curse and demanded that he infect Benjamin with TSK-17 within forty-eight hours. These are direct Furykov disclosures; later Chapter 415 analysis is not imported backward.
- **Guardian Spirit Beast curse theory:** Furykov fears his curse could eliminate or neutralize Benjamin’s Guardian Spirit Beast ability and proposes reflecting the curse back at himself. Benjamin expands that logic into a theory that death plus the beast could let him transcend kingship and become a godlike protector. No curse reflection or god-state is demonstrated in Chapter 413.
- **Combo Master:** Furykov’s Nen ability is introduced by the name **Combo Master**. The supplied synopsis says the ability is explained but does not include those mechanics, so the archive records the **name and owner only**. Activation, effect, condition, range, cost, and other mechanics are deliberately left unresolved rather than imported from Chapter 415 or outside material.
- **Secret Window:** Benjamin uses inherited **Secret Window** on Camilla. Chapter 413 adds that extended use lets Benjamin access what Musse had seen before death, which gives Benjamin knowledge that Camilla is a counteractive-type Nen user. Broader limits remain unresolved.
- **Special Martial Law orders:** Benjamin schedules Special Martial Law for **2:15 p.m.**, orders preparation at **2:00 p.m.**, tells his soldiers to execute other princes’ personal guards on sight, spares Hunter Association members, orders princes toward **Room 1001**, and reserves Camilla and Tserriednich for himself. These are orders and preparatory movements, not completed outcomes.
- **Marayam:** Furykov says Marayam’s location remains unknown. Benjamin says to leave the matter to Saquelle and use Rihan if the group does not report to Room 1001 by 2:30 p.m. Saquelle remains in prose only because the current canonical entity graph has no maintained Saquelle node.
- **Stopping point:** At 2:00 p.m., Benjamin’s soldiers receive their orders. **Kanjidol draws a knife and Coventoba begins to panic.** No Chapter 414+ consequence is imported.
