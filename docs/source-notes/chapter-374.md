# Chapter 374 source notes

Source basis: user-supplied Hunterpedia Chapter 374 synopsis, chapter notes, and drawing-error notes on 2026-08-08.

## Confidence boundaries

- Room 1013 is still observed in two incompatible states: Hanzo experiences an empty room while Vergei and Marayam's household remain in an occupied, communicative state.
- Kurapika's explanation that the occupied quarters were transported/spatially displaced by Nen is a theory, not a confirmed mechanism.
- Kurapika's suspicion that Marayam's Guardian Spirit Beast is producing a defensive spatial mechanism is also a theory at this chapter boundary.
- Rihan's classification of Salé-salé's Guardian Spirit Beast as a diffusive-induction Manipulator and his smoke/clone propagation model are recorded as Rihan's analysis, not omniscient confirmation of every rule.
- Fugetsu's door-and-tunnel translocation is observed, but the supplied text does not give the official ability name or full conditions. No name is invented in the dedicated Chapter 374 record.
- Zhang Lei's Guardian Spirit Beast has produced two confirmed coins by this chapter boundary. Both are described as marked with the number 1; their effects remain unknown.
- The supplied synopsis states that a large layout summarizes each prince, queen, servant/guard count, and Hunter guards, but the actual table values were not supplied. The maintained record acknowledges the panel without reconstructing missing counts.

## Drawing errors

### Page 10: Fugetsu door handle

An earlier version drew the outgoing door handle on the right. The full release corrected it to the left. The archive should follow the corrected full-release orientation.

### Page 14: Yushohi and Ryoji

The chapter art shows Yushohi and Ryoji guarding Kacho in her bedroom even though they are assigned to Fugetsu. This error remained in the later volume release.

Archive rule: **do not treat the panel placement as a reassignment.** Yushohi and Ryoji remain assigned to Fugetsu unless a separate textual source explicitly changes their assignment.
