Source basis: user-supplied Hunterpedia Chapter 377 synopsis and chapter notes.

## Lovely Ghostwriter and Neon Nostrade

Chapter 377 confirms that **Lovely Ghostwriter has disappeared from Chrollo's Skill Hunter book**.

The supplied chapter notes say this **heavily implies Neon Nostrade's death**, but the supplied material does not contain a direct death scene or an independent formal statement that Neon is deceased.

The maintained archive therefore records:

- ability disappearance: confirmed;
- Neon death: strongly implied, not directly confirmed at the Chapter 377 boundary.

No later source is imported backward to strengthen that conclusion.

## Death Note reference

The supplied notes flag Shizuku's explicit comparison of Chrollo's book to a **Death Note** as an anachronism: the Succession Contest chronology is described as occurring in 2001, while the real-world *Death Note* manga began publication in December 2003.

This is stored as publication/source metadata only. It is not used to infer any story-world date change or Nen mechanic.
