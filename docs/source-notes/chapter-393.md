# Chapter 393 source note

## Source boundary

The maintained Chapter 393 packet is based only on the user-supplied Hunterpedia Chapter 393 synopsis and trivia text supplied on 2026-08-09. No chapter title was supplied, so the archive stores no invented title.

Chapter 393 directly continues the Voyage Day 10 lower-tier threads from Chapter 392. No exact Chapter 393 clock time is supplied, so none is inferred.

## Luini and the Phantom Troupe

Luini begins the chapter alive and directly confronting Nobunaga, Phinks, and Feitan at the **Tier 5 Cha-R office**. He proposes cooperating to eliminate Cha-R, Xi-Yu, Kakin’s royal family, seize the Black Whale, return to the old world, and destroy it.

Nobunaga immediately rejects the proposal and kills Luini with his katana. The chapter later shows Luini’s decapitated and bisected corpse. Luini is therefore recorded as **dead at Chapter 393**.

The older coarse event record placed the confrontation on Tier 3. Chapter 393 corrects that event location to the Tier 5 Cha-R office.

After Luini’s death, the Troupe states that it will destroy Heil-Ly while continuing the Hisoka search. Heil-Ly/Morena intelligence can be acted on before the Hisoka hunt when useful, but the Hisoka objective is not abandoned.

No post-mortem continuation of Luini’s spatial ability is established.

## Luini’s Nen type

Perigord says he is confident Luini was an **Emitter**. This is a character judgment, not an independently confirmed classification in the supplied synopsis. The archive therefore keeps Luini’s Nen type **unknown** at the Chapter 393 boundary.

## Heil-Ly member disclosures and ability development

Chapter 393 discloses the following Heil-Ly member information:

- Daemon: level 20, construction worker; Nen type unsupplied.
- Gelato: level 19, professional gamer; Nen type unsupplied.
- Perigord: level 18, investigator’s assistant; Nen type unsupplied.
- Bille: level 20, arcade employee; Nen type unsupplied.
- Voconte: level 26, human trafficker, confirmed **Emitter**.
- Matvere: level 18, college student; Nen type unsupplied.

The members discuss raising their levels so they can reach **level 21** and develop abilities. They also recall Morena’s teaching that each person has an **innate Nen type**. Matvere wanting to become an Emitter is therefore not treated as evidence that Nen type can be freely selected.

Gelato declines Morena’s offer to reveal her type because she wants it to remain a surprise. Her type stays unresolved.

## Voconte’s door ability

Voconte proposes using his unnamed “door” ability to catch prey who enter a trap. The archive uses **Voconte’s Door Ability** only as a descriptive label because no official name is supplied.

Voconte’s natural Nen type is confirmed as Emitter. That does **not** automatically make the door technique an Emission ability. The technique’s own category, activation, placement rules, trigger, range, duration, transport/capture behavior, target limits, aura cost, and reset conditions remain unresolved.

## Morena’s counter-design example

Morena tells the members to analyze opponents and use creativity, including restricting either the enemy or the enemy’s ability. She gives a hypothetical example of an ability that tells the user how many hits are required to defeat an opponent.

That is **ability-design advice**, not a revealed Morena ability, not a new Heil-Ly technique, and not added to the canonical ability graph.

## Apparent Hisoka and Chapter 405 retrospective knowledge

Lynch and Zakuro recover alive after the Chapter 392 encounter. They remain convinced the man they encountered was Hisoka. Hinrigh proceeds to the Tier 3 cinema and negotiates with the same man under that working assumption.

The supplied Chapter 392 note says Chapter 405 later reveals that this man was Bonolenov disguised as Hisoka through Metamorphorsen. That is later knowledge and is **not backfilled into Chapter 393**.

Accordingly, the Chapter 393 runtime graph:

- describes him as an **apparent Hisoka / man believed to be Hisoka**;
- does not objectively tag `Hisoka Morow` as the participant in the cinema negotiation;
- does not tag `Bonolenov` as the participant;
- does not attach `Metamorphorsen` to the Chapter 393 encounter;
- leaves objective identity unresolved at the Chapter 393 boundary.

Hinrigh offers Tier 1/VVIP access and asks the man not to initiate a fight with the Troupe until Heil-Ly is dealt with. The man accepts while reserving the right to fight if the Troupe attacks first. This is a temporary non-initiation arrangement, not permanent peace.

## Ken’i, Maizan, and Fourth Prince approval

Ken’i learns about Maizan’s intelligence deal with Hinrigh and matches the **50-million** offer. He also proposes a **5-million** payment to the finder and corporal for potential Heil-Ly identifications and discusses arranging a culprit/eyewitness cover story.

Ken’i says Xi-Yu and Cha-R are cooperating to crush Heil-Ly and that they have approval from the Fourth Prince. Chapter 393 does not independently show Tserriednich granting or defining that approval in the supplied scene, so the archive preserves it as **Ken’i’s statement**.

Maizan’s thought that everyone Ken’i smiles at dies remains a rumor/joke in the scene. It is not treated as a Nen ability, curse, or factual death rule.

## Room 3101

Connelly, identified as a Xi-Yu lieutenant, brings the money. Maizan then leads Hinrigh, Ken’i, and the mafia group to **Room 3101**, a canonical Tier 3 first-class-cabin location already present in the ship graph.

Maizan says a worker told him Room 3101 was the only room without a bathroom wall because plumbing space was behind it. A wall being present is treated as suspicious evidence worth testing. Ken’i explicitly notes that anything could be behind it because the room is at the edge of the ship. The chapter therefore does not yet establish Heil-Ly ownership merely from the wall anomaly.

An unnamed old man opens the room. No name is invented for him.

Maizan enters first and then disappears from Hinrigh and Ken’i’s view. Chapter 393 does **not** establish:

- where Maizan went;
- whether he was teleported, moved through another space, concealed, or affected by some other mechanism;
- who owns or activates the mechanism;
- whether Maizan is dead, unconscious, or physically unharmed;
- whether Room 3101 definitively connects to Heil-Ly;
- the trigger conditions or complete route rules.

Hinrigh throws a knife through the doorway and observes that the knife does **not** disappear. One knife test is not generalized into a rule that objects are immune, only people can be transported, or any other complete mechanism.

**Maizan is not marked dead, and the knife does not disappear in the observed Chapter 393 test.** Maizan’s life/body/consciousness state is recorded as unresolved after disappearance, while the Room 3101 mechanism remains open.
