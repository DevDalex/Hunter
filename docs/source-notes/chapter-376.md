# Chapter 376 source note

Source basis: user-supplied Hunterpedia Chapter 376 synopsis and chapter notes.

## Fugetsu door timing wording

The supplied synopsis says that Fugetsu **confirms that her ability activates once per day** after the door manifests again on the fourth voyage day.

The supplied chapter notes phrase the same point more cautiously: Fugetsu **theorizes that the door can only appear once a day, or perhaps every 24 hours**.

The maintained archive therefore records the second manifestation as confirmed, while preserving the exact reset rule as unresolved at the Chapter 376 boundary. It does not collapse “calendar day” and “rolling 24 hours” into one mechanic.

## Boundary rule

No later-chapter explanation of the door system is imported into Chapter 376. The observed fact that choosing a destination does not immediately make the exit appear is stored separately from Fugetsu’s timing theory.
