# Chapter 390 source boundary

Reviewed: 2026-08-09

## Source policy

The sole story source for this maintained Chapter 390 integration is the user-supplied Hunterpedia Chapter 390 synopsis. No chapter title was supplied, so no title is invented. Outside story claims and later-chapter outcomes are excluded from the Chapter 390 knowledge boundary.

## Chronology

- Chapter 390 opens directly on Coventoba reacting to the **1 → 10** coin change that ended Chapter 389.
- The integration therefore retains **Voyage Day 10** continuity from that immediate handoff.
- The supplied Chapter 390 synopsis gives no exact clock time. No clock time is invented.

## Zhang Lei coin boundary

Chapter 390 adds two important observations without resolving the coin system:

- Coventoba recognizes the **same aura** on the coin now displaying 10 that he associated with the original coin.
- Tenftory simultaneously holds a separate coin still displaying **1**.

Coventoba briefly considers whether the 10 coin could be a different object, but the aura match is his evidence for continuity. The archive preserves that observation without inventing a separate identity-verification mechanic.

Coventoba considers putting the coin into Zhang Lei’s Guardian Spirit Beast’s mouth and decides the experiment is too risky. Because he does not perform the experiment, **no result or rule is recorded**.

Zhang Lei’s idea that the coins could be distributed to the population after he becomes King is stored as his own future-use theory, not an established Nen mechanic.

The following remain unresolved at Chapter 390:

- what causes the number to change;
- what the number represents;
- whether time, possession, transfer, gifting, secrecy, or another factor controls progression;
- the activation threshold;
- the eventual effect on a holder.

## Zhang Lei / Onior boundary

Zhang Lei visits Onior and asks about Guardian Spirit Beasts and Nen.

Onior says he knows nothing about Guardian Spirit Beasts and is not presented here as personally knowledgeable about Nen. He does know younger Xi-Yu members who know Nen and agrees to consult them for Zhang Lei.

The supplied synopsis explicitly describes Zhang Lei as entrusting the matter to **his father, Onior**. The archive records the father-son relationship but does not infer additional biological, inheritance, succession-eligibility, or Seed Urn consequences from it.

Zhang Lei says three siblings have already died in the succession battle. The supplied synopsis here does not enumerate the three, so this integration does not invent an unstated list.

## Xi-Yu operational state

Onior orders Hinrigh to:

- search for Hisoka on Tier 3;
- permit the Phantom Troupe to search Tier 4;
- kill Morena while the operation proceeds.

Onior hopes controlled Tier 4 access will help Xi-Yu influence the Phantom Troupe’s movement. This is tactical accommodation, **not** permanent alliance, trust, or proof that Xi-Yu can actually command the Troupe.

Hinrigh recruits Lynch and Zakuro for the field operation.

## Heil-Ly civilian-registration boundary

The encountered Heil-Ly members on Tier 3 are described as officially registered on the Black Whale as **civilians rather than Mafia members**.

Hinrigh recognizes that an open Mafia-versus-civilian fight would create serious consequences and stops Lynch and Zakuro’s confrontation.

The archive limits this fact to the encountered group. Chapter 390 does not establish that every Heil-Ly member on the ship uses identical registration paperwork.

## Bloody Mary

**Bloody Mary** is explicitly named as Zakuro Custard’s ability.

The supplied synopsis directly shows:

- Zakuro receiving a deep cut to the neck;
- Zakuro saying he needed more blood;
- Zakuro thanking the attacker for cutting him because he could not do so himself;
- Zakuro then using Bloody Mary to subdue the opponent.

This establishes a demonstrated blood-linked use. It does **not** establish the complete Nen category, exact blood-manipulation method, range, maximum usable volume, duration, or a universal rule that every activation requires an enemy-inflicted wound.

## Body and Soul

**Body and Soul** is explicitly named as Lynch Fullbokko’s ability.

Lynch attacks an opponent, asks questions through the ability, and obtains basic information about Heil-Ly’s goal and Morena’s ability.

Chapter 390 therefore confirms successful interrogation/information acquisition. The archive does not inflate that into a universal truth-compulsion rule or invent exact wording, range, duration, resistance, or Nen-category rules absent from the synopsis.

## Hinrigh transformation boundary

The supplied Chapter 390 synopsis does **not** provide the formal name of Hinrigh’s ability.

The archive therefore uses the descriptive label **Hinrigh Object-to-Animal Transformation** rather than inventing an official ability name.

The demonstrated facts are:

- Hinrigh touches two soldiers’ guns;
- the gun barrels later become live snakes;
- the snake mouths retain the guns’ firing function;
- the resulting gunfire kills both soldiers.

Nen type, maximum transformed mass, transformation duration, target count, aura cost, selection rules, and whether all transformed objects preserve all original functions remain unresolved.
