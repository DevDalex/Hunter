# Chapter 408 source note

## Source boundary

The modern Chapter 408 packet uses the current user-supplied Hunterpedia-style synopsis provided on 2026-08-12 as its sole substantive Chapter 408 story source. The previously maintained packet already carried the title **Negotiation: Part 2** and Japanese title **交渉②** from user-supplied Hunterpedia material; those metadata fields are retained rather than re-fetched from another source.

Chapter 408 immediately continues the Tier 2 Morena/Borksen negotiation begun on Voyage Day 12 in Chapter 407. No new exact clock time is supplied. The strict stopping point is the shipwide announcement that **Special Martial Law has been declared and is not a drill**. The chapter does not supply the declaration's cause, issuing authority, enforcement sequence, effect on the negotiation game, or later outcome, so those remain quarantined to Chapter 409+.

## Card-label continuity

Chapter 407 names the seven parent cards Aim, Power, Question A, Question B, Yes?, No?, and Deal. The supplied Chapter 408 synopsis calls the ability-explanation card **Ability**. The archive treats **Power / Ability** as a translation-label alias for the same parent-card slot rather than creating an eighth parent card.

The Chapter 408 parent-card sequence is:

1. **Aim**
2. **Power / Ability**
3. **No?**

The child-side response sequence is:

1. **Joker** revealed and discarded
2. **Yes** revealed and discarded
3. **X** revealed and discarded

At the stopping point, **No** and **Return** remain. The final response is unresolved.

## Aim / Morena identity boundary

Under Aim, Morena states that her immediate goal is the destruction of the Kakin Empire and that she intends to continue afterward until humanity is destroyed. Borksen says that answer is insufficient and presses for the origin of Morena's worldview.

Morena then says that she is **not the original Morena Prudo**. The original royal Morena, a Second-Track Faker, is said to be buried in the grave associated with the current Morena identity. The current Morena invokes Carne Levare and lèse-majesté until Borksen recognizes her as a **Carnival Orphan**. The current Morena's birth name is not supplied and is not invented.

## Carne Levare / lèse-majesté boundary

The chapter narration defines **Carne Levare**, “Farewell to the Flesh,” as a secret royal carnival held irregularly in a randomly selected Kakin village. Villagers are sorted into Entertainers and Others. The chapter also explains historical and modern forms of lèse-majesté punishable by death, including severe reproductive and genetic restrictions involving royal descendants.

Morena says her mother was forced to entertain visiting royalty, became pregnant, and died when Morena was two. Morena says Carnival Orphans were scarred at birth and routed into an institution connected in her account to Heil-Ly human trafficking and an underground hospital. Children were sorted into Second-Track Fakers or **flesh**; Morena says she was classified as flesh and endured that role for about twenty years before becoming Heil-Ly's boss.

Morena identifies herself with the **'98** Carnival Orphan group and says seven later groups entered during the following twenty years, including a **'20** group. She uses that recurrence after Kakin democratization as the basis for her claim that Kakin has not changed and will not change. The archive preserves the distinction between the chapter's narrated institutional framework and Morena's autobiographical claims where appropriate.

## Borksen ideological boundary

Borksen listens with sympathy but does not adopt Morena's ideology. She reflects on Kakin's repression, military schooling, national contradictions, and the ordinary life she expected to live. Despite its flaws, she considers Kakin her home. She explicitly rejects murder as a solution and internally resolves to escape the extraordinary situation rather than join Morena's project.

That internal rejection is important: Chapter 408 does **not** establish a Heil-Ly alliance, voluntary ideological conversion, or Nen awakening for Borksen.

## Power / Ability and Contagion boundary

Borksen says she has only recently heard of Nen and understands it broadly as superpowers connected to the succession battle. Morena explains that Nen can ordinarily be learned through training and says her own ability can awaken compatible recruits without requiring the same long training period.

Morena describes herself as a dealer or **mother** to up to **twenty-two child players** who share the larger goal. She says children gain points, develop individualized abilities with her support, and can eventually become parents who create additional children. She compares the expansion model to a pyramid scheme. Previously established Contagion point thresholds remain historical archive knowledge, but Chapter 408 does not need to restate every old threshold to preserve the new explanation.

Morena says she listens to each child's goals, personality, attributes, and state of mind while helping shape ability development. No Chapter 408 scene shows Borksen actually being initiated, awakened, assigned an ability, or becoming a child in the system.

## Specialization boundary

When Borksen challenges how Morena can know she is important before developing an ability, Morena says Borksen is a **Specialist**. Morena says an unnamed Heil-Ly **Enhancer** strengthened his sense of smell enough to identify Nen categories from the faint aura of even unawakened people, and that this person identified Borksen and continues searching the ship for Specialists.

The supplied synopsis does **not** identify this Enhancer. The archive therefore does not equate him with Dogman or any other named Heil-Ly member.

Morena says her own research suggests roughly **1 in 3000** people are Specialists, while explicitly acknowledging possible inaccuracies. She estimates that a ship the size of the Black Whale could contain roughly **50–60** more. She also says the detector encountered awakened Specialists, including a famous **Heavens Arena Floor Master**. The Floor Master is unnamed and remains unnamed in the archive.

Morena explains Specialization as unusually flexible for difficult hybrid or category-defying abilities and says that she herself is a Specialist. These Nen-theory statements are recorded as Morena's explanation within the negotiation rather than converted into broader unsupplied universal mechanics.

Morena says she has a specific ability request for Borksen but withholds it because it belongs to the prospective team-role discussion under **Yes?**. The requested ability remains unresolved at the Chapter 408 boundary.

## Yes removal / anti-cheating inference boundary

After Orarge shuffles the four remaining child cards, Borksen reveals **Yes**. Morena and the surrounding Heil-Ly members look disappointed because final Yes is no longer available.

Borksen treats their disappointment as evidence that they do not appear accustomed to simply cheating the game, but she immediately limits the inference: she may be the first candidate who genuinely does not want to join. The archive therefore records **Borksen's bounded inference**, not narrator proof that cheating has never occurred and not a Nen-enforced anti-cheating rule.

## No / X / vows boundary

Under **No?**, Morena distinguishes No from X.

- **No** is described as an irrevocable refusal. Heil-Ly will not recruit Borksen again, but she and the people around her become outsiders rather than allies and therefore potential points in Heil-Ly's killing/progression campaign. Morena also says Borksen cannot simply be released under No after learning their secrets.
- **X** voids the negotiation. Borksen keeps her memories, but Heil-Ly promises to treat the negotiation as though it never happened, avoid renewed recruitment or deliberate contact where possible, and avoid the people Borksen values. Morena qualifies that, given her ultimate goal, those people are postponed rather than granted permanent immunity.

Borksen asks why Morena would accept such a dangerous X condition. Morena explains **vows and limitations**, saying meaningful risk can produce greater Nen return. She says accepting genuine recruitment failure strengthens the support her ability can provide and the unity of the resulting team. The archive records this explanation without inventing a numerical multiplier, automatic death formula beyond the supplied consequences, or invisible enforcement mechanism.

## Final response state / stopping point

Borksen reveals **X** as the third child response card. She sees the remaining cards as **No = death-equivalent** and **Return = survival-equivalent** from her immediate strategic perspective.

An alarm then sounds. Morena and Borksen look up as **Special Martial Law** is declared and the announcement emphasizes that it is not a drill.

That announcement is the Chapter 408 stopping point. At the boundary:

- Aim, Power / Ability, and No? have been used;
- Joker, Yes, and X are in the child graveyard;
- No and Return remain;
- Borksen has not joined Heil-Ly;
- Borksen has not awakened Nen;
- Morena has identified Borksen as a Specialist but has not disclosed the specific ability she wants Borksen to develop;
- the unnamed category-detection Enhancer remains unnamed;
- the unnamed Heavens Arena Floor Master remains unnamed;
- the cause and implementation of Special Martial Law remain unknown at this chapter boundary;
- **no Chapter 409+ card resolution, martial-law aftermath, movement, or later explanation is imported backward into Chapter 408**.
