## Gypsy Life: Bohemian Rhapsody

Chapter 417 names Benjamin’s Guardian Spirit Beast ability **Gypsy Life: Bohemian Rhapsody**.

The supplied mechanics are:

1. after Benjamin dies, his Guardian Spirit Beast fuses with **Benjamin Baton**;
2. it then becomes the Guardian Spirit Beast of one of Benjamin’s blood relatives;
3. the right to select the **first** future host is determined between Benjamin and his Guardian Spirit Beast;
4. after that, the side with selection authority **alternates** whenever another host must be chosen.

No actual death, fusion, or host transfer occurs within Chapter 417.
