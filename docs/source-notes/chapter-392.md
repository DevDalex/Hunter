# Chapter 392 source note

## Source boundary

The maintained Chapter 392 packet is based on the user-supplied Hunterpedia Chapter 392 synopsis, trivia, and note text supplied on 2026-08-09. No chapter title was supplied, so the archive stores no invented title.

Chapter 392 directly continues the Voyage Day 10 lower-tier operation from Chapter 391. No exact clock time is supplied in the synopsis, so none is inferred.

## Padaille and Misha Hao

Padaille remains dead after Hinrigh killed him in Chapter 391. His corpse appearing to rise, speak, and walk away in the public scene is not recorded as revival, restored consciousness, or a reversal of the Chapter 391 death state.

Chapter 392 resolves Hinrigh’s earlier statement that he was counting on Misha. Misha Hao is a deceased Xi-Yu member and former family undertaker. Her post-mortem Nen causes her to appear and inconspicuously dispose of a person killed by a Xi-Yu member; once the corpse is dealt with, she vanishes. Padaille is the demonstrated cleanup target.

The synopsis supplies no official ability name or Nen category for Misha’s effect. `Misha Hao’s Post-Mortem Disposal Ability` is therefore an explicit descriptive archive label, not a claimed canonical technique name. Trigger timing, range, visibility, corpse-control method, disposal destination/method, cost, exceptions, and other limits remain unresolved.

## Corporal Maizan’s secret-room lead

Maizan offers Hinrigh information concerning Morena’s possible location. Hinrigh offers 50 million if the information is reliable; Maizan demands at least 30 million upfront. Maizan says workers wired a room absent from the Black Whale plans and that the person responsible for the ship’s wiring told him to stay quiet when he asked about workers who had been silenced.

Maizan then **guesses** that the unplanned room must belong to Heil-Ly if Xi-Yu does not have a secret hideout. The archive keeps this as Maizan’s inference and sales lead, not confirmed Heil-Ly ownership or a confirmed Morena location. Hinrigh likewise requires Maizan to personally lead him to the location before treating the intelligence as verified.

## Bloody Mary and Body and Soul

Zakuro’s Bloody Mary drops continue the Tier 3 search. Chapter 392 shows the drops pointing Lynch and Zakuro toward multiple candidates; this does not establish an infallible identity-recognition rule.

Lynch punches Hanal with Body and Soul after asking whether he is Hisoka. Hanal’s inner soul answers that he is not, directly demonstrating the ability’s identity-question use.

Lynch later tries Body and Soul on a man she and Zakuro believe is Hisoka. The punch seemingly fails and Lynch abruptly falls; the man calls his counter a reflex. The chapter does not establish why this happens. The archive does not invent aura resistance, speed, a counter-ability rule, or any other mechanism. Lynch later groans and is therefore alive at this boundary.

## The apparent Hisoka and the Chapter 405 retrospective reveal

Within Chapter 392, Lynch and Zakuro identify the man they find as Hisoka. Zakuro reaches that conclusion after sensing the man’s overwhelming aura and seeing Lynch effortlessly subdued.

The user-supplied trivia/note separately states that **Chapter 405 later reveals** this man to have been Bonolenov disguised as Hisoka with Metamorphorsen under Chrollo’s orders.

That Chapter 405 reveal is retrospective knowledge. It is not backfilled into Chapter 392’s contemporaneous event graph, character knowledge, or ability state. Accordingly:

- the Chapter 392 encounter is described as involving an **apparent Hisoka / man believed to be Hisoka**;
- the false-identity encounter does not objectively tag `Hisoka Morow` as the target;
- it does not tag `Bonolenov` as a Chapter 392 event participant;
- it does not attach `Metamorphorsen` to Chapter 392 ability mechanics;
- Chapter 405 remains the boundary where that identity reinterpretation becomes available.

## Tsudonke and shipment logistics

The kiosk woman tells Tsudonke that a Tier 1 general store can arrange requested goods through upper-class contacts. She states Voyage Day 14 as the last air-shipment order deadline and says the high-speed-boat deadline is three days earlier. She also says a small item might be drone-delivered and that connections/cash could affect practical flexibility.

These are her logistics statements, not a universal guarantee that every item or customer can use every method.

Tsudonke gives himself four days to find Hisoka so he can ask for proper autograph paper as his reward.

## Ken’i Wang’s strategy boundary

Ken’i makes Hisoka the Cha-R search priority and orders personnel not to approach Hisoka before he arrives. He hopes that negotiation with Hisoka can be used to make Hisoka, Heil-Ly, and the Phantom Troupe damage one another while Cha-R preserves balance.

Ken’i also believes the Heil-Ly hitman’s marking condition has already been fulfilled and interprets Heavens Arena footage as showing that Hisoka likes stacking the deck against himself. Both are character assessments. They are not converted into universally confirmed ability mechanics or guaranteed future outcomes.

## Luini and the Phantom Troupe

Luini probes the Cha-R Tier 5 office through spatial openings and then directly confronts Nobunaga, Phinks, and Feitan. The chapter ends with Nobunaga drawing his katana and threatening him.

Luini is **alive at the Chapter 392 boundary**. No later combat result or death is imported into this chapter. The synopsis demonstrates spatial openings and contains Ken’i’s marking-condition assessment, but it does not supply enough information here for the archive to invent a formal ability name or exhaustive spatial mechanics.
