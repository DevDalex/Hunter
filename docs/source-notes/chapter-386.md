# Chapter 386 source boundaries

Reviewed: 2026-08-09

Sole story source for the dedicated Chapter 386 upgrade: user-supplied Hunterpedia Chapter 386 synopsis.

## Chronology

Chapter 386 explicitly takes place on **Voyage Day 9**.

The supplied synopsis gives no exact clock time for any scene. The archive therefore records Day 9 without inventing hours or minutes.

## Theta's luminol result

Salkov tests the place where Theta remembers seeing Tserriednich's body after the Chapter 385 headshot. He reports that luminol detects **no blood** there.

This is important new forensic evidence. It supports the conclusion that the apparent corpse did not behave like an ordinary physical gunshot death scene.

It does **not** yet establish that every part of Theta's experience was simply an illusion, nor does it reveal Parallel Future's complete later mechanics.

At the Chapter 386 boundary the archive still withholds:

- a ten-second duration;
- a complete precognition model;
- the full sequence Tserriednich consciously perceives;
- a complete rule for what other observers perceive;
- the full divergence mechanism.

Parallel Future therefore receives a Chapter 386 knowledge override rather than inheriting later mechanics from the broad archive.

## Justice Bureau location correction

The supplied Chapter 386 synopsis explicitly places Melody in the **Justice Bureau on Tier 2**.

An older broad foundation record placed the Justice Bureau on Tier 1. Chapter 386 supplies a direct tier location, so the canonical location layer now corrects the Justice Bureau to Tier 2 and remaps maintained Justice location-history references to the corrected facility.

This correction is treated as a location-data repair, not as a new movement by every character previously associated with Justice.

## Keeney's note and the twins' account are evidence claims, not independent truth

Keeney's suicide note says he acted alone in the escape attempt.

The two girls being questioned say Keeney forced them onto the lifeboat.

Kaiser says he lacks evidence proving or disproving whether they were forced or whether the escape was their own plan.

The archive therefore preserves all three layers separately:

1. Keeney's sole-responsibility claim;
2. the girls' forced-escape account;
3. Justice's unresolved evidentiary position.

None is silently promoted into an independently verified reconstruction of the Chapter 383 escape.

## Kacho ontology remains fixed after Chapter 383

Biological Kacho died during the failed lifeboat escape in Chapter 383.

Without You, Kacho's Guardian Spirit Beast, continues in Kacho's form beside Fugetsu.

Accordingly, any Chapter 386 reference to Kacho being questioned with Fugetsu is recorded as the **Kacho-form Without You continuation**, not as the biological prince returning to life.

## Melody's protective-custody interpretation

Melody realizes that the extended Justice questioning is keeping her away from princes who have invited her to their suites.

The archive records the protective function as Melody's interpretation of the situation unless independently established by the investigator's own statement. The existence of the invitations and the ongoing questioning are explicit.

## Halkenburg's four consciousness models

Halkenburg's group treats the consciousness operating Shikaku's body as Sumidori and monitors Sumidori's original sleeping body.

They explicitly consider four possibilities for Shikaku's original consciousness:

1. it is gone and Shikaku is dead;
2. it moved into Sumidori's original body;
3. it coexists with Sumidori inside Shikaku's body;
4. it exists somewhere or in someone else.

These are **candidate models**. Chapter 386 does not establish which one is correct.

## Halkenburg's additional hypothesis is not supplied

The synopsis states that Halkenburg shares another hypothesis with his followers, but no dialogue or content for that hypothesis is supplied.

Halkenburg himself says he is unsure whether it is possible because he does not fully understand Nen and wants to consult an expert.

The archive therefore records only that an additional hypothesis exists. It does not reconstruct the missing argument from later chapters or outside sources.

## Shikaku-body suicide and Sumidori wakeup

The consciousness treated by Halkenburg's group as Sumidori operates Shikaku's body, goes to Luzurus's door, shouts loyalty to Benjamin, and shoots the Shikaku body through the head.

The archive distinguishes body and consciousness state:

- **Shikaku's original body:** dead after the self-inflicted gunshot;
- **Shikaku's original consciousness:** unresolved;
- **Sumidori's original body:** wakes after the Shikaku body dies;
- **consciousness in Sumidori's awakened body:** not yet confirmed at the supplied endpoint.

Halkenburg asks for the awakened person's post and service number specifically to verify identity. The synopsis ends before the answer, so the archive does not preemptively declare that Sumidori's consciousness definitely returned to his own body.

## Benjamin and Balsamilco's attribution

Balsamilco reports that Shikaku killed himself and says Halkenburg's ability most likely caused it. Benjamin and Balsamilco then treat Halkenburg as their greatest immediate threat.

The suicide and their threat reprioritization are explicit. Their causal attribution is recorded as their assessment rather than as a newly revealed complete ability rule.

## Kurapika's Emission inference

Kurapika and Bill feel the distant aura rumbling. When it stops abruptly, Kurapika infers that the event may have been an **Emitter attack** and notes the danger of attacks that can penetrate barriers.

He does not know who attacked whom.

The archive therefore does **not** use this remote inference to reclassify Halkenburg's collective possession arrow as confirmed Emission. Its maintained Chapter 386 Nen classification remains bounded by the direct evidence available for that ability.

## Water Divination and Kurapika's class strategy

Kurapika introduces Water Divination and demonstrates a result he identifies to the class as Specialization.

He selects Ladiolus after she produces enough aura to take the test. Babimyna interprets the choice as a deliberate motivational signal that Nen aptitude is not determined by gender or physical strength.

Kurapika also explains that:

- Nen types should not be disclosed publicly;
- tests will occur privately with Kurapika and Bill;
- their assistance is necessary to meet the two-week training deadline;
- the students' Nen-type information is payment for the class and compensation for Kurapika's risk;
- empowering rival bodyguards is intended to prolong the stalemate among princes;
- anyone unwilling to accept those terms may leave.

## Tubeppa and Woble Guardian Spirit Beast observations

Furykov and Babimyna discuss the two beasts' continued nonappearance to Benjamin's observers.

Tubeppa's beast is described as refusing to show itself while Rihan focuses on it.

Woble's beast has also not appeared. Babimyna proposes two possibilities:

- Woble may be too young;
- the beast may be a counterattacking type.

Neither explanation is confirmed in Chapter 386.

## Tserriednich's sub-second Zetsu target

Tserriednich tells Salkov that he is working on shortening the response time required to enter Zetsu and that they will spar once he can do so in **less than one second**.

The archive records this as a training target. It does not claim that Tserriednich has already achieved sub-second Zetsu in Chapter 386.

His remark that he now finds two-faced women cute is preserved as a behavioral statement. It is not converted into proof that he trusts Theta, forgives her, or fully understands her attempted assassination.

## Excluded later knowledge

The Chapter 386 maintained layer intentionally excludes unsupplied Chapter 387+ information about:

- Parallel Future's complete mechanics;
- the final answer to Halkenburg's identity check;
- the final location/state of Shikaku's consciousness;
- later refinements of the possession-arrow transfer rules;
- later consequences of Tserriednich's Guardian Spirit Beast warning;
- later Water Divination results;
- later revelations about Woble's or Tubeppa's Guardian Spirit Beasts.
