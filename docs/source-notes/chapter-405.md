## Real Hisoka / fake Hisoka boundary

The man gambling in the Tier 1 VIP casino is the real Hisoka. Square-3 rules are explained by spectators, and Hisoka reveals five 3s and two aces after asking for two additional cards. He later hits triple sevens on a slot machine and walks away without claiming the prize.

Hisoka's thoughts about Chimera Ants, the shipwide rumbling, group Nen, Guardian Spirit Beast support, the Seed Urn ceremony, and his preference for one-on-one lethal intimacy are retained as Hisoka's own appraisal and preferences. The archive does not convert those thoughts into a universal rule about what kinds of opponents can interest him.

The "Hisoka" seen by Lynch, Zakuro, and the mafia search in the earlier lower-tier sequence is revealed in Chapter 405 to have been Bonolenov using a transformation disguise. This later reveal resolves the apparent-Hisoka identity question without rewriting Chapters 392–393 as if their participants already knew the answer.

## Chrollo / Bonolenov decoy plan

A flashback shows Chrollo instructing Bonolenov to transform into Hisoka and remain where mafia personnel are likely to notice him. Chrollo expects the established mafia to move a dangerous wild card into better-controlled upper-tier accommodations to preserve balance and face, thereby creating room for the Troupe to search for the real Hisoka.

Chrollo predicts that the real Hisoka may already be on Tier 1 as a VIP through a prince's invitation. He tells Bonolenov that if Bonolenov encounters the real Hisoka, he should change into someone else and wait for Chrollo to decide whether a fight occurs.

Bonolenov infers that Chrollo kept this Tier 1 prediction from the other members because Chrollo wants to deal with Hisoka personally. That motive attribution remains Bonolenov's inference unless independently established elsewhere.

## Metamorphorsen boundary

Chapter 405 expands Bonolenov's previously known transformation ability. The archive retains the established canonical ability ID/name **Battle Cantabile: Metamorphorsen** and records **Transformation: Metamorphosen / Metamorphosen** as supplied aliases rather than silently replacing the older catalog name.

The supplied mechanics are:

- Bonolenov can transform into a person he has talked to in person;
- the available transformation time equals the amount of time he has spent talking with that person;
- narration states that there is no body-size limit;
- Bonolenov believes that the smaller the transformed body is compared with his own, the shorter the duration he can maintain it.

The smaller-body duration relationship is explicitly preserved as Bonolenov's belief rather than promoted into a narrator-confirmed formula. Chapter 405 does not supply the Nen category, aura cost, cancellation rule, whether copied voices/biometrics are perfect in every case, or a general combat-use rule.

Bonolenov transforms from the false-Hisoka appearance into Owl in a nearby Tier 1 casino bathroom. His comment that the ability is poorly suited to fighting Hisoka and that he could not comfortably transform while evading Bungee Gum is his tactical assessment, not a new Bungee Gum mechanic.

## Lynch / Zakuro retrospective

Chapter 405 reveals that Lynch's Body and Soul did not simply fail against the apparent Hisoka. Lynch strikes disguised Bonolenov while asking whether he is Hisoka; Bonolenov hears his own inner voice disclose that he is only pretending to be Hisoka. The retrospective establishes that Lynch's interrogation effect successfully penetrated the disguise.

Zakuro's reaction shows that he did not hear the compelled inner-voice answer. Bonolenov uses that observation to conclude that the answer was limited to Lynch and the target in this demonstrated use. The archive does not universalize the exact audible audience beyond the supplied scene.

Bonolenov knocks Lynch out reflexively, knocks Zakuro unconscious after confirming he did not hear the answer, transforms into Zakuro, and gets the recovering Lynch to disclose that she knows the Hisoka was fake. Lynch threatens to report the impostor to Hinrigh.

Bonolenov's present-day thoughts directly reveal that he subsequently killed Lynch, used Lynch's form to deceive Zakuro, and led Zakuro toward the movie theater before separating. Lynch's death therefore belongs to Chapter 405's knowledge boundary while its physical occurrence is retrospective to the earlier Tier 3 encounter. Zakuro remains alive.

The archive does not invent Lynch's exact killing method, exact body-recovery time, corpse location beyond the supplied lower-tier context, or a new Bonolenov combat ability.

## Cha-R / Phantom Troupe analysis

At the Cha-R Tier 5 hideout, Nobunaga asks Ken'i Wang and Tajao whether they truly know the current Heil-Ly. Ken'i explains that Morena only recently became boss, is a royal descendant without succession rights, and that Kakin mafia boss titles are inherited by royal-blood descendants who have no claim to the throne.

Nobunaga compares the hidden-base behavior he witnessed with the Phantom Troupe's own leader-strategy/member-tactics structure. He concludes that Morena's group is organized despite individual members acting freely.

Nobunaga then argues that Heil-Ly's civilian killing makes more sense if the group intends to destroy everything aboard the ship, including the royal family and Kakin's existing order. Phinks and Feitan add a vows-and-limitations model in which a kill count could serve as a condition for accumulating powerful Nen.

Those statements are retained with speaker attribution. Chapter 405 demonstrates Contagion leveling and later Dogman's developed capacity, but Nobunaga/Phinks/Feitan's complete motive-and-sacrifice model is not converted into a newly proven universal Nen formula.

Tajao contrasts established Kakin mafia culture with Morena's group: he describes the traditional families as balance-oriented, dependent on civilians as customers, and willing to fight only as a last resort. He then declares that Cha-R and Xi-Yu will fully support the three Troupe members in crushing Heil-Ly.

Nobunaga, Phinks, and Feitan proceed with Tajao through a long dark route. The chapter ends as Tajao opens another door and warns that conditions beyond it will be rough. The archive does not identify the unseen area, exact geometry, destination, or Chapter 406 consequence.

- sniff a person's Nen type from **five meters** away;
- determine whether a person has learned Nen from **two meters** away;
- identify the relevant Nen information with **100% certainty** by sniffing the person's head at close range.

The chapter does not supply an official ability name, Nen category, exact smell/aura mechanism, target throughput, obstruction rules, or duration. A descriptive archive ability label is used only to index the demonstrated function.
