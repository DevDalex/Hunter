# Chapter 401 source note

## Source boundary

The modernized Chapter 401 story packet uses the current user-supplied Hunterpedia-style Chapter 401 synopsis provided on 2026-08-10 as its sole substantive story source. The pre-existing chapter-title catalog is used only to retain the English title **Moonlight**. No Japanese title is supplied in the current synopsis, so none is invented. No Chapter 402+ story claim is used to fill a Chapter 401 gap.

Chapter 401 is present-day Voyage Day 10 material. The Room 1014 sequence begins at exactly **11:45 a.m.** and the Beyond/Kanzai coda occurs at exactly **2:00 p.m.** Intermediate clock times are not supplied and are not invented. Longhi narrates events beginning roughly thirty years earlier, but those narrated historical disclosures are not assigned invented calendar dates.

## Longhi / Silent Majority boundary

Longhi states directly that she already knows Nen and does not need Water Divination. She also states that the snake ability associated with the Room 1014 killings does **not** belong to her. Chapter 401 therefore excludes Longhi as the Silent Majority user according to her direct statement, but it does not identify the actual user.

Furykov begins the sequence believing Longhi is already a Nen user and hoping she will eliminate Woble even at the cost of her own life. He later continues to interpret Longhi as the likely killer before reconsidering the inference after the closed-door meeting. These are Furykov’s internal judgments and wishes. They are not narrator confirmation that Longhi used Silent Majority, killed the prior victims, or entered the room intending to attack Woble.

## Transparent Words—Moonlight Act

Longhi reveals **Transparent Words—Moonlight Act** as her conditional **Manipulation** ability. She manifests a pen and paper made of aura as its contract interface. The archive records the supplied Manipulation classification and does not infer an additional Conjuration category merely from the visible pen-and-paper manifestation.

The demonstrated framework requires the other party to sign a limited-term contract voluntarily; Longhi describes voluntary signature as an absolute condition. Her vow and limitation require her to explain the applicable information in advance and without deceit. She distinguishes accurate disclosure of an ordinary contract’s terms from the more extensive explanation required before she can grant the special reward power tied to her true plan.

The exact Chapter 401 peace agreement binds Tubeppa’s side and Woble/Oito’s side not to interfere with or attack the opposing Prince, Queen, or affiliated guards. It expires at **9:00 a.m. the following Sunday** and automatically renews for one additional week if a messenger from Tubeppa is inside Room 1014 at that moment.

Longhi says Moonlight Act activates on violation of this agreement and that the punishment is **one week of enforced Zetsu**. Kurapika points out that “attack” is ambiguous because Nen treatment or aid could also be described as an attack. Longhi answers that, absent evidence of fraud, damage, or conduct clearly intended to cause harm, Tubeppa will not unilaterally invoke termination and Longhi will not arbitrarily trigger her ability. The archive treats this intended-harm/fraud/damage standard and the one-week Zetsu penalty as terms of the demonstrated Chapter 401 agreement, not as automatic universal rules for every Moonlight Act contract.

The chapter does not establish Moonlight Act’s maximum physical range, maximum number of simultaneous contracts, total aura cost, all cancellation routes, or whether every possible contract can grant a borrowed use of the ability.

## Dowsing Chain boundary

Kurapika draws Dowsing Chain while hearing Longhi’s disclosure and later notes that the chain is not moving. He takes this as evidence that Longhi has not lied during the conversation. The archive records **Kurapika’s no-lie reading**.

That reading is not promoted into an omniscient truth certificate. Dowsing Chain is already documented as capable of being misled by altered memories, manipulation, and similar epistemic problems. More importantly, Longhi herself labels several conclusions as speculation. A motionless chain therefore does not transform her beliefs about curse targets or a possible Beyond-child prince into narrator-confirmed facts merely because she appears to believe them sincerely.

## Longhi / Beyond parentage

Longhi reveals that she is the biological daughter of **Beyond Netero**. At the Chapter 401 boundary this biological parentage is treated as a disclosed fact, distinct from her later theory about Beyond having a child among the royal princes.

Longhi says that roughly thirty years earlier Beyond convinced young Kakin officers to participate in fake marriages. According to her account, the wives bore Beyond’s children while the legal husbands and wives raised those children as their own and enrolled them in the Royal Military Academy. Longhi guesses that Beyond repeated the process as new princes were born and guesses that most of the prepared children eventually entered the intended royal-military positions. Those frequency and success-rate estimates remain Longhi’s inference rather than exact program statistics.

Longhi says she discovered that she lacked traits she expected from her legal/adoptive father and then learned the truth from her adoptive parents. She assumes most of her half-siblings were never told their birth circumstances or the existence of the wider sibling group. The archive does not convert that assumption into a confirmed census of who knows what.

Longhi also recounts that her parents repeatedly described Beyond’s goals in terms that she regarded as consistent with Kakin’s world-unification policy. That is preserved as Longhi’s recollection and interpretation of what she was taught, not as proof that the Kakin state officially knew, ordered, or endorsed Beyond’s hidden biological/curse program.

## Nen awakening from birth

Longhi states that she and the prepared siblings had their Nen abilities awakened from birth as a consequence of Beyond’s Nen attack. She explains that newborns in this situation instinctively avoid excessive fatigue and develop techniques such as Ten and Zetsu, and she connects her own long-term physical performance to this condition.

The archive does not universalize this explanation into a rule for every Nen-awakened infant or every natural Nen prodigy. Chapter 401 supplies Longhi’s explanation of **Beyond’s prepared children**, not a general medical law of Nen development.

## Makaha / curse-sacrifice discovery

Longhi identifies **Makaha** as her paternal half-sister through Beyond. Makaha had expected to serve under Camilla, but Camilla’s promotion of marginalized Have-Nots into personal-soldier positions prevented that placement. Makaha became suspicious because her parents reacted with unusual indifference to the failed placement, and Longhi says the two later investigated while both were soldiers under Queen Duazul.

Longhi says the Seed Urn Ceremony helped them recognize that they shared a “seal.” A renowned Nen user examined it. According to Longhi’s account of that examination, each prepared child is possessed by powerful malevolent Nen that is released upon the bearer’s death. Longhi exposes a seal beneath her tongue and states that she and Makaha are **two of ten “strong curse sacrifices”** selected to curse targets to death.

Longhi estimates that there may be an equal or larger group of weaker curse sacrifices because Beyond could not have known the final number of princes. That weaker-sacrifice population and its size remain speculation. The chapter confirms ten selected strong sacrifices through Longhi’s disclosure, not an exact total census of every prepared child.

The examining Nen user is said to have judged the visible seal exceptionally powerful and impossible to purge merely by burning the surface or cutting away the marked tissue. The archive records resistance to those physical attempts. It does **not** convert that statement into proof that every Nen exorcist or every possible exorcism method must fail.

The individual curse abilities, exact assigned targets, target-selection process, range, timing beyond the disclosed death-release, and removal/exorcism route remain unresolved.

## Prince-target and activation theories

Longhi reasons that because some prepared children, including Makaha, failed to become trusted guards of their originally expected prince without any obvious corrective action from Beyond, occupying a particular trusted-soldier position may not be required for the curse plan. She says the princes are likely curse targets because there are more than ten strong sacrifices and fourteen princes, but explicitly acknowledges that this is speculation. The archive therefore does not assign a confirmed prince target to Longhi, Makaha, or any other prepared child at Chapter 401.

Bill proposes two models: Beyond may control an activation switch, or activation may be left automatic as a limitation that strengthens the curse. Kurapika considers the automatic model more likely. Neither model is demonstrated or confirmed.

Kurapika then speculates that Beyond may want Benjamin to become king and imagines a strategy in which Benjamin could be secretly warned and rely on Special Martial Law as an ace. This is **Kurapika’s hypothesis**. Chapter 401 does not show Beyond endorsing Benjamin, does not establish that Benjamin knows about the curse network, and does not show Special Martial Law being declared or active.

## Possible Beyond child among the princes

Longhi rejects Kurapika’s Benjamin-centered model as inconsistent with how she understands Beyond’s ambition and says she believes **one of the participating princes is Beyond’s biological child**. This remains Longhi’s hypothesis.

Oito confirms that succession eligibility is limited to the “children of legal wives” of King Nasubi. The wording creates a possible loophole in which legal-wife maternity does not itself settle biological paternity. It does **not** prove that a participating prince is Beyond’s child, and Chapter 401 identifies no prince as such.

The archive therefore keeps three distinct statements separate: Longhi is Beyond’s confirmed biological daughter; Oito confirms the legal-wife eligibility wording; and the existence/identity of a Beyond-child prince remains unresolved.

## Tubeppa treaty and investigation reward

Longhi’s treaty is a peace agreement between Tubeppa’s camp and Woble/Oito’s camp. Neither side is to interfere with or attack the other side’s Prince, Queen, or affiliated guards under the stated intended-harm standard. The weekly duration, messenger renewal, and one-week enforced-Zetsu breach penalty are explicit.

Longhi adds a reward condition for Kurapika: determine whether any participating prince is Beyond’s child before Tubeppa withdraws from the agreement or only two princes remain. If no prince meets the criteria, the condition is still fulfilled. If Tubeppa formally terminates the agreement before completion, that treaty-linked condition expires, but Longhi promises that Kurapika may continue the investigation and later enter a separate personal contract with her for the reward.

The reward is **one use of Moonlight Act by Kurapika**. Chapter 401 does not show Kurapika receiving or using that borrowed activation yet.

Longhi then reveals her current true plan: if her hypothesis is correct and Beyond’s child is one of the princes, **Longhi intends to kill that prince herself**. The assassination intent is confirmed as Longhi’s stated conditional plan. The victim remains unidentified because the underlying royal-parentage hypothesis remains unresolved.

## Kurapika’s cover story / Furykov analysis

Sakata interrupts the long private meeting. Kurapika temporarily exits and tells the waiting observers that peace negotiations with Tubeppa are taking longer because of disagreement over the terms. After the private agreement is completed, Kurapika again tells the group that the sides are open to agreement but have not found common ground and disagree about how much information to share.

The internal scene establishes that this public account conceals the actual compromise. The archive therefore treats Kurapika’s statement as an operational cover story, not as evidence that the treaty failed.

Furykov analyzes the lack of a fight, Longhi’s tears, Kurapika’s aura, and several hypothetical ability structures. He cannot narrow the possibilities and eventually doubts the simple Longhi-as-assassin explanation. None of his remote-ability, multi-step-ability, overpowering-condition, or non-Nen-negotiation possibilities becomes a confirmed mechanic.

Kurapika and Bill next take Tenftory behind closed doors and continue the controlled awakening system. Furykov concludes that the procedure itself protects Woble because a conspiracy accusation would be difficult to prove and stopping the classes might strengthen group unity. He asks Babimyna whether he also wants reassignment; Babimyna hesitates and says nothing is wrong. The reason for Babimyna’s hesitation remains unresolved.

## Beyond / Kanzai coda

At **2:00 p.m.**, Beyond remains seated in his Tier 1 cell with a book. Kanzai refuses to exchange the book and later refuses pen and paper on security grounds. Beyond and Kanzai joke about whether the book itself could be used as a weapon.

Beyond then says there is someone he wants to talk to and asks Kanzai to arrange a meeting. The requested person is **not named** in the supplied Chapter 401 synopsis, and Chapter 401 does not show Kanzai actually arranging or completing the meeting. No target identity is invented.

## Stopping point

At the Chapter 401 boundary:

- Longhi is confirmed as an existing Nen user and is excluded as the Silent Majority user according to her direct denial; the actual user remains unknown.
- Transparent Words—Moonlight Act is confirmed as a conditional Manipulation contract ability with voluntary signature and truthful advance-disclosure requirements.
- The Tubeppa–Woble/Oito peace agreement has an explicit weekly renewal structure and a one-week enforced-Zetsu breach penalty.
- Longhi is confirmed as Beyond Netero’s biological daughter.
- Longhi and Makaha are confirmed paternal half-sisters and two of ten strong curse sacrifices in Beyond’s prepared network.
- The exact curse abilities and individual targets remain unknown; prince-target attribution remains Longhi’s theory.
- Bill’s activation-switch models and Kurapika’s Benjamin/Special-Martial-Law scenario remain hypotheses; Special Martial Law is not active.
- Oito confirms the legal-wife eligibility wording, but no participating prince is confirmed as Beyond’s biological child.
- Kurapika’s motionless Dowsing Chain means no lie was detected in the exchange; it does not turn sincerely held speculation into objective fact.
- Longhi conditionally intends to kill Beyond’s child if that child proves to be one of the princes.
- Kurapika conceals the successful private compromise from the outside observers while the Room 1014 closed-door awakening system continues.
- At 2:00 p.m. Beyond requests a meeting with an unnamed person; the target and outcome remain unresolved.
- No Chapter 402+ consequence, curse assignment, royal-parentage reveal, or later alliance development is backfilled.
