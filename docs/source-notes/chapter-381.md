# Chapter 381 source-boundary notes

Source basis: user-supplied Hunterpedia Chapter 381 synopsis and chapter notes only.

## Hunter Code is retrospective metadata

The Chapter 381 call to Melody outwardly tells her to assist Keeney in preventing Kacho and Fugetsu from escaping.

The supplied Chapter 381 notes explain, using a rule revealed later in Chapter 383, that the hidden Hunter Code message is:

**Assist Princes escape fully**

That decoded phrase is retained in the archive as a retrospective annotation. It must **not** be projected backward as explicit knowledge that Chapter 381 characters or the Chapter 381 reader already possess.

The supplied notes describe a translation difference:

- Official Viz version: the opening time check is **11:55**, and the hidden message is reconstructed from the eleventh word of each following sentence.
- Japanese text: the time is **13:44**, and the corresponding mechanism selects clauses rather than the English word-based structure.
- The communicated hidden meaning is stated to be the same.

## Voyage timing

The chapter explicitly gives **8:00 p.m. on Voyage Day 5** for the shipwide announcement that the stowaway has been captured and the lockdown is lifted.

The earlier Chapter 381 scenes are stored as preceding Day 5 events, but no more precise timestamp is invented for them.

## Fugetsu route

Chapter 381 confirms that Fugetsu is absent from Room 1011 and that ordinary corridor footage does not explain her movement. Melody treats the incident as confirmation of the transportation nature of Fugetsu's Guardian Spirit Beast.

The archive therefore records teleportation as strongly supported while keeping the complete route/activation mechanics unresolved at the Chapter 381 boundary.

## Melody's manipulation suspicion

Melody hears a strange heartbeat from the Kakin jurisdiction supervisor and considers the possibility that he is being manipulated by Nen.

This is stored as **Melody's suspicion only**, not as confirmed Manipulation.

## Rihan timing analysis

Rihan estimates roughly seventy hours before the influence becomes visible in Koroabde and roughly eight hours for Salé-salé's closest associates to reach maximum affection.

These are retained as Rihan's observational estimates, not universal fixed thresholds for every target.

## Stinger Ball

Chapter 381 confirms only that Yushohi has successfully attached **Stinger Ball** to Fugetsu. Its trigger, effect, range, removal method, duration, and Nen classification are not supplied and are therefore left unresolved.
