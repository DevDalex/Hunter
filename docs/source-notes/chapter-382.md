# Chapter 382 source boundaries

Reviewed: 2026-08-09

Sole story source for the dedicated Chapter 382 upgrade: user-supplied Hunterpedia Chapter 382 synopsis.

## Title metadata

The supplied material does not include the English, Japanese, or romanized chapter title. The dedicated record therefore keeps all title fields null rather than inventing or importing a title.

## Nasubi survival statement

Halkenburg fires at Nasubi and the bullet stops inches before the King, but the supplied synopsis does not identify the mechanism responsible for stopping it. Nasubi then says that he is part of the ritual and cannot die until the ritual ends, and tells Halkenburg that three shots would be needed if he truly intended to kill him.

The archive preserves those statements as **Nasubi's claims**. It does not convert them into a fully verified universal rule of the Seed Urn ritual, and it does not attribute the stopped bullet to Nasubi's Guardian Spirit Beast or another mechanism without source support.

By contrast, when Halkenburg turns the gun on himself, the synopsis explicitly attributes the stopped bullet to **Halkenburg's Guardian Spirit Beast**. That interception is recorded as directly observed.

## Halkenburg collective arrow

The supplied synopsis explains the first demonstrated attack in detail but does not provide an official ability name. `Halkenburg collective possession arrow` is therefore a descriptive archive label, not a claimed canonical title.

Confirmed in Chapter 382:

- followers' aura functions as powerful protection around Halkenburg;
- followers' aura forms the arrow while Halkenburg's aura forms the bow;
- the arrow pierces Shikaku's Culdcept card shield;
- once the bow is drawn, the synopsis states that the arrow cannot be intercepted or blocked;
- a successful hit robs/suppresses the host's original will in exchange for the body of one Halkenburg follower;
- one follower collapses and Shikaku's body rises and asks Halkenburg for orders.

The complete consciousness-transfer topology, exchanged follower identity, duration, recovery rules, and official Nen-type classification remain unresolved at this chapter boundary.

## Salé-salé assassination

Salé-salé is shown unresponsive and apparently not breathing while attendants attempt CPR. Yushohi reports to Benjamin that the assassination succeeded.

The supplied synopsis does **not** name the ability or exact killing method. The archive therefore confirms the successful assassination but does not assign **Stinger Ball** as the murder method merely because Yushohi was previously shown using Stinger Ball against Fugetsu.

## Chronology

The chapter explicitly reaches **8:00 p.m. on Voyage Day 8** at the Sunday banquet opening. Earlier Chapter 382 scenes are recorded as preceding that timestamp without inventing exact clock times.
