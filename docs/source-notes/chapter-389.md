# Chapter 389 source boundary

Reviewed: 2026-08-09

## Source policy

The sole story source for this maintained Chapter 389 integration is the user-supplied Hunterpedia Chapter 389 synopsis. No chapter title was supplied, so no title is invented. Outside story claims and later-chapter outcomes are excluded from the Chapter 389 knowledge boundary.

## Chronology

- The chapter opens in a flashback at **10:00 a.m. on Voyage Day 9**.
- The story returns to the present at **11:30 a.m. on Voyage Day 10**.
- The Day 10 11:30 a.m. return overlaps the fourth aura-rumbling endpoint already recorded in Chapter 388.
- The later Tyson, Luzurus, Camilla, and Zhang Lei scenes occur in the present, but the supplied synopsis gives no exact clock times for them. No times are invented.

## Shikaku / Halkenburg theory boundary

Kanjidol proposes that an ability associated with Halkenburg directly forced Shikaku to kill himself. Balsamilco challenges the proposed route, motive, manipulation logic, and prince-killing implications.

The following remain **character theories**, not confirmed Chapter 389 mechanics:

- Kanjidol’s direct-causation explanation for Shikaku’s suicide.
- Kanjidol’s possible Emission attack against Benjamin.
- Benjamin and Balsamilco’s theory that Guardian Spirit Beasts are restricted by royal law from directly killing princes or one another, insofar as Chapter 389 presents this as their deduction.
- Benjamin’s conclusion that Halkenburg’s attack probably requires his entire team.
- Balsamilco’s proposed one-ally-sacrificed-per-attack rule.
- Kanjidol’s later Duazul-guard diversion/manipulation scenarios.
- Basho’s private theory that Benjamin’s side staged a post-mortem-Nen red herring around Shikaku.

The archive therefore does not treat Chapter 389 as a complete reveal of Halkenburg’s consciousness-transfer topology.

## Vict and Tackle Shield

Vict’s radio transmission at Day 10 11:30 a.m. is fragmented. It establishes his involvement in the Halkenburg confrontation and includes references to failure, Halkenburg, a bow, and apparent invincibility before a scream.

**Tackle Shield** is explicitly named as Vict’s ability. Chapter 389 does not supply its Nen type, activation rule, exact shielding/tackling effect, range, duration, aura cost, or the precise reason it fails.

Benjamin inspects four star marks on his hand, confirms Shikaku’s death, and says Vict remains alive. The archive preserves that observation without inventing a new universal decoding rule for every star mark.

## Halkenburg custody state

A five-person Restricted Voyage Permit Agency special task force, including Steiner and Peuckert, takes Halkenburg into custody. Halkenburg remains alive.

Balsamilco says Halkenburg cannot contact his supporters until after trial. He expects insufficient evidence may lead to release under surveillance, and Benjamin’s side plans to make its move around the trial. Those future results are not marked completed in Chapter 389.

## Tyson / Giuliano boundary

Giuliano is emotionally absorbed in the Book of Tyson, revises his view of Tyson, and cries during the household’s deliberately early birthday celebration.

Chapter 389 **does not establish that his feelings were caused by Nen, Tyson’s Guardian Spirit Beast, or a Book of Tyson manipulation effect**. The archive records the emotional attachment as a character state, not an ability demonstration.

## Camilla Have-Not curse network

Chapter 389 directly explains:

- ancient Kakin afterlife companions;
- the Have-Not caste and the continuation of caste restrictions after the burial custom ended;
- Camilla admitting Have-Nots to her personal army, providing housing, and granting military-equivalent status and rights;
- the revival of the afterlife-companion tradition as a Nen curse-assassination system;
- one assigned curse bearer per other remaining prince;
- carrying a target-linked object and repeatedly cursing the target;
- longer preparation and closer death proximity strengthening the curse;
- burning the object, drinking an infusion of its ashes, and suicide by dagger as the terminal ritual;
- aura deprivation after activation;
- enforced Zetsu and death after several hours at the strongest described level;
- Camilla’s side maintaining a Nen exorcist and investigating whether enemies have exorcists.

The six-month estimate given for Kacho and Fugetsu after their detention is treated as **specific to that presented situation**, not a universal curse duration formula.

Fukataki’s statement that the practical chance against a Guardian-Spirit-Beast-protected prince requires suicide directly in front of the prince while making eye contact is stored as the group’s operational assessment, not silently generalized into an absolute law for every curse interaction.

## Sarahell timing boundary

In Chapter 389, Sarahell is assigned to Woble and **plans** to attend Kurapika’s next Nen class to obtain proximity.

She is not recorded as physically present inside Room 1014 at the Chapter 389 boundary. Any later class entry remains later knowledge.

## Zhang Lei coin boundary

Tenftory reports successful Nen training, can now see Zhang Lei’s Guardian Spirit Beast, and receives a coin openly from Zhang Lei.

Coventoba privately knows he acquired a coin earlier and observes that its number has changed from **1 to 10**.

Chapter 389 does not explain:

- what the number means;
- what caused the change;
- whether time, possession, gifting, secrecy, or another factor is responsible;
- the activation threshold;
- the eventual effect on a holder.

Those mechanics remain unresolved at this boundary.
