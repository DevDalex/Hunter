## Strict Chapter 414 boundary

Luzurus anticipates Special Martial Law, orders absolute nonresistance, consolidates household weapons, directs Ridge to occupy Kanjidol, and orders drugs/plants destroyed. ### Room 1009

Yushohi describes his own En/Gyo limitations and hypothetical threat models, including En-triggered abilities and an En-plus-emitted-projectile pair; those examples are not observed enemies. He concludes that the expanding Nen-user field makes **Stand By Me** increasingly impractical for its original assignment. No breach, occupant-resolution scene, expiry, injury, death, or detention is imported.

The synopsis note reports a translation/editorial discrepancy in which one Yushohi referent appears as “Princess” in the original publication and “Queen” in Viz, with a later author comment correcting the intended referent to **Prince**. The maintained Chapter 414 semantic referent is Prince. This note is used only to resolve wording and does not import Chapter 416 story content.

His ideas about an Association pardon, stopping the Succession Contest, weakening Kakin’s monarchy, elevating unaffected princes as symbols of a new era, and likely V5/V6 support are Bill’s strategic/political proposals. They are not treated as Hunter Association, Kakin, or V5/V6 decisions.

### Woble search and curse-response framework

Pyon’s search has no official match. Daughter Woble’s actual location remains unknown. Kurapika’s statement that an off-ship Woble would be “invincible” is strategic target-priority language, not literal supernatural invulnerability. Kurapika separates two practical routes: unravel Beyond’s curse and identify/lift the cursed-child network, or find Woble and seek an exorcist. He also distinguishes attacking the source of a curse from removing its target, chooses the source-focused route because resources are limited and it aligns with Longhi’s contract, and continues under the preventable worst-case assumption.

Chapter 414 does not establish contact, acceptance of a mission, or arrival by either friend. Oito then reveals that a maternal-side relative works at a post office in **Yamato**, allowing a coded letter sent without a return address to be returned with an address filled in, entirely off record. **That disclosure is the strict Chapter 414 endpoint.** No postcard dispatch, returned address, relocation, confinement, Luzurus disappearance, or other Chapter 415+ result is imported.
