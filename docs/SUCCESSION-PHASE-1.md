# Succession Phase 1 — Chapters 414–415 reconciliation

Phase 1 advances maintained Succession Contest research from Chapter 413 through Chapter 415 without redesigning the broad arc page or beginning the full Voyage Timeline completion phase.

## Active source hierarchy

1. **VIZ** controls official English publication identity and release verification.
2. **Hunterpedia** provides the project's structured chapter references and entity cross-links.
3. **VoraciousDrake** is used selectively for independent translation and terminology cross-checking.
4. **Succession Contest Encyclopedia V2** is community context for cast, households, and systems only. It predates Chapters 414–415 and is not evidence for their events.
5. **HunterxNen** is community context for the Nen system only. Manga and chapter-specific records remain controlling.

Comic Watch, unapproved Japanese-language analysis pages, and nen.ca/mangahelpers are excluded from the active Chapter 414–415 source set.

## Maintained Chapter 414 boundary

- Luzurus's pre-martial-law nonresistance and evidence-removal strategy.
- Separate tracking of the actual Woble and the substitute infant shown in Room 1014.
- Passenger-record search logic without claiming a resolved location.
- Oito's coded-contact authorization and Kurapika's trusted-friends contingency.
- No claim that Gon or Killua were contacted, assigned, or committed.
- The stated 49-day voyage marker remains a chapter marker rather than a manufactured exact calendar calculation.

## Maintained Chapter 415 boundary

- Furykov's Fullmetal Alchemist / Combo Master record.
- Beyond's prince-linked death-triggered curse network and decades of aura accumulation.
- The 365-day decoding and 700-day antidote estimates remain specific to the detected curse.
- The 13:50 coded-postcard operation and 14:15 special-martial-law activation.
- Tubeppa's relocation order, Luzurus's missing status, Marayam's isolated-space hold, and Oito's conditional confinement.
- Unknown responsibility, incomplete relocations, and unresolved emergency objectives remain explicitly uncertain.

## Product changes

- Detailed research boundary: Chapter 413 → Chapter 415.
- Voyage timeline: 134 → 146 selected events.
- Chapter records, royal states, assignments, relationships, Nen records, body states, mysteries, evidence links, and search coverage updated together.
- Previous dossier and timeline datasets remain available as compatibility modules.

## Excluded from Phase 1

- Broad Succession Contest arc-page rewrite.
- Comprehensive every-scene Voyage Timeline audit.
- Stable canonical event-ID migration.
- Structured time fields, state snapshots, and cause-and-effect mode.
- Remaining Succession overview/research visual-baseline redesign.
