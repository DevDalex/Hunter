## Hour 48 — Ability and system dossiers

Ability dossiers now present mechanics through a six-stage model:

1. trigger;
2. range;
3. target;
4. duration;
5. cost;
6. limitation.

They also preserve conditions, known uses, owners, system memberships, linked events, certainty, first-known chapter, source evidence, and the distinction between demonstrated, partial, and unrevealed mechanics.

System dossiers now present rules, costs, risks, unresolved questions, linked abilities, Guardian Spirit Beasts, connected actors, and evidence as one chapter-bounded architecture.
