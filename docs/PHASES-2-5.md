# Product Phases 2–5

## Phase 2: Reading experience

- Mission-based archive entry instead of an automatic redirect.
- Persistent official, readable, indexed, and selected chapter context.
- Four explanation-depth contracts for chapter material.
- Spoiler-safe saved reading boundaries.

## Phase 3: Connected intelligence

- Chapter-to-chapter state diff engine.
- Shared comparison schemas for characters, princes, organizations, abilities, and chapters.
- Claim semantics separating canon, inference, theory, editorial analysis, and translation notes.
- Controlled certainty vocabulary and first-class open-question records.

## Phase 4: Research workspace

- Local-first bookmarks and reading position.
- Saved investigation boards.
- Shareable filtered research snapshots.
- JSON and CSV exports with explicit exclusion of protected chapter-image binaries.
- Reusable research citation formatting.

## Phase 5: Scale and polish

- Route, data, media, and interaction performance budgets.
- A capability registry describing the maintained product contract.
- Integrated product audit covering Phases 2–5.
- Progressive domain adoption rather than parallel replacement systems.

## Integration rule

Workspaces should consume the shared modules under `src/lib/succession/` and `src/data/` rather than defining local versions of boundaries, certainty labels, comparisons, bookmarks, or export policy.
